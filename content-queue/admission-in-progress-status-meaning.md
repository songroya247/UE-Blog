<section id="understanding-the-jamb-caps-admission-system">
<h2>Understanding the JAMB CAPS Admission System</h2>

<h3>What is JAMB CAPS?</h3>
<p>The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board established the Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) to digitize higher education entry in Nigeria. Before CAPS, institutions issued admission offers manually. That older model created multiple admissions for single candidates while leaving other qualified applicants stranded.</p>
<p>CAPS serves as a centralized platform. It coordinates candidate credentials, institutional recommendations, and final approval records. Every legitimate offer from a university, polytechnic, or college of education must clear through this portal.</p>

<h3>The Central Importance of Your CAPS Dashboard</h3>
<p>Your portal dashboard serves as the authoritative source for your admission profile. It reflects your UTME choices, Post-UTME screening records, and uploaded O'Level grades.</p>
<p>If a school publishes your name on an internal noticeboard but your profile remains blank on CAPS, your admission is incomplete. Paying acceptance fees without an official offer on CAPS exposes you to registration risks during matriculation.</p>

<h3>How to Access Your CAPS Dashboard</h3>
<p>Accessing CAPS on a mobile device requires a specific browser adjustment. Mobile layouts often hide the sidebar menu.</p>
<ol>
<li>Open your mobile web browser (such as Chrome or Safari).</li>
<li>Visit the <a href="https://efacility.jamb.gov.ng">JAMB eFacility Portal</a>.</li>
<li>Log in with your registered email and password.</li>
<li>Enable <strong>Desktop Site</strong> mode in your browser menu.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Check Admission Status</strong> and click <strong>Access my CAPS</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> If your screen remains stuck on a blank "Welcome" page, turning on "Desktop Site" mode in your browser settings will reveal the left-hand navigation bar immediately.</p>
</div>

<section id="decoding-admission-in-progress-aip">
<h2>Decoding "Admission in Progress" (AIP)</h2>

<h3>The Core Meaning: Recommendation and Active Validation</h3>
<p>When your status displays "Admission in Progress," your application has moved past initial applicant pools. Your institution recommended you for admission, and your application is undergoing final validation through the CAPS platform before an admission decision is finalized.</p>
<p>At this stage, system algorithms and clearance desk officers review your profile. They ensure your details meet minimum statutory guidelines before releasing an offer.</p>

<h3>Is "Admission in Progress" a Positive Indicator?</h3>
<p>Yes, seeing this status is an encouraging sign. It shows that you cleared the department's aggregate cut-off score. Out of thousands of applicants, the institution chose your candidate profile.</p>
<p>While you are not fully admitted yet, you have cleared the most competitive step in the selection process.</p>

<h3>What "Admission in Progress" Does NOT Mean</h3>
<p>Misinterpreting this status leads to costly errors. "Admission in Progress" does <strong>not</strong> mean:</p>
<ul>
<li>Your admission letter is ready for printing.</li>
<li>You should pay institutional acceptance fees immediately.</li>
<li>Your admission is 100% guaranteed regardless of credential accuracy.</li>
</ul>
<p>If validation reveals subject mismatches or missing O'Level grades, the recommendation can be returned to the institution.</p>

<section id="how-institutions-and-jamb-process-recommendations">
<h2>How Institutions and JAMB Process Recommendations</h2>

<h3>Candidate Sorting and Departmental Evaluation</h3>
<p>Generally, after Post-UTME registration closes, an institution's ICT department processes aggregate scores. Screening results combine with UTME performance and SSCE grades to generate an aggregate rank.</p>
<p>Departments then evaluate candidates against available lecture space and laboratory capacity. Candidates who meet departmental requirements are shortlisted for recommendation.</p>

<h3>Institutional Approval and Upload to CAPS</h3>
<p>Once departmental lists are prepared, they move through the institution's internal admissions committee. Depending on the institution, the Vice-Chancellor, Rector, or Registrar approves the finalized batch.</p>
<p>The institutional admissions officer then uploads the shortlist to the CAPS portal for verification.</p>

<h3>Policy Frameworks: Merit, Catchment Area, and ELDS</h3>
<p>Federal universities have historically operated admissions using Merit, Catchment Area, and Educationally Less Developed States (ELDS) categories in accordance with prevailing government policy.</p>
<p>Implementation and specific percentage allocations may vary depending on current regulations and individual institutional guidelines.</p>

<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> State-owned and private institutions follow different policy guidelines set by their respective governing councils and state education ministries.</p>
</div>

<h3>JAMB Clearance and Final Status Updating</h3>
<p>When the list lands on CAPS, system verification begins. JAMB validates that your admission complies with its requirements and that all required records are complete.</p>
<p>Once validation passes without error, the status updates on your dashboard from "Admission in Progress" to "Admitted."</p>

<section id="high-intent-candidate-questions">
<h2>High-Intent Candidate Questions</h2>

<h3>Why is Admission in Progress taking so long?</h3>
<p>Delays usually occur when an institution submits candidate recommendations in large batches. Verification takes time when thousands of profiles undergo automated compliance checks simultaneously.</p>
<p>Another frequent cause is incomplete candidate records on the central portal, particularly unverified O'Level uploads.</p>

<h3>Does Admission in Progress guarantee admission?</h3>
<p>It is a strong indicator, but it is not an absolute guarantee. Your offer becomes final only when the portal changes to "Admitted" and you click "Accept." If discrepancies arise during compliance checks, the status can revert.</p>

<h3>Can Admission in Progress change back to Not Admitted?</h3>
<p>Yes, this can happen. If compliance checks reveal that a candidate lacks required core credits or used an incorrect UTME subject combination, the recommendation is declined.</p>
<p>In such cases, the candidate profile shifts back to "Not Admitted" or becomes eligible for alternative course placements.</p>

<h3>Can Admission in Progress change overnight?</h3>
<p>Yes. Status updates occur in real time as clearance desk officers process batches. Profile statuses frequently change overnight or during early morning hours.</p>

<h3>Can I pay acceptance fees while my status says Admission in Progress?</h3>
<p>No. You should refrain from paying acceptance fees until your portal displays "Admitted" and you officially accept the offer on CAPS. Paying before clearance can result in non-refundable financial losses if your recommendation encounters errors.</p>

<h3>What happens after I click Accept Admission?</h3>
<p>Clicking "Accept Admission" locks in your offer on the central portal. Once accepted, the system enables the option to print your official JAMB Admission Letter.</p>
<p>You can then proceed with paying your institution's acceptance fee and completing physical screening.</p>

<div class="callout warn">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
<p><strong>Warning:</strong> Selecting "Reject Admission" declines the offer permanently. That specific offer cannot be restored once rejected, and your profile enters the Marketplace pool for alternative choices.</p>
</div>

<h3>How often should I check CAPS?</h3>
<p>Checking your profile once every 48 hours is sufficient during active admission periods. Refreshing the portal multiple times an hour does not speed up administrative validation.</p>

<section id="practical-real-world-admission-scenarios">
<h2>Practical Real-World Admission Scenarios</h2>

<h3>Scenario 1: My friend is admitted, but my profile still displays Admission in Progress</h3>
<p>Institutions process candidates in separate batches. Your friend’s name may have been included in an earlier list, while your profile is part of a subsequent batch undergoing validation.</p>

<h3>Scenario 2: The school published an admission list online, but CAPS remains unchanged</h3>
<p>Schools occasionally publish internal lists before CAPS finishes processing the upload. Give the portal 3 to 7 working days to synchronize with institutional data.</p>

<h3>Scenario 3: My status reverted from Admission in Progress to Not Admitted</h3>
<p>This situation points to a compliance issue during verification. Check if your UTME subjects match your chosen course or verify whether your SSCE result was properly uploaded to the portal.</p>

<h3>Scenario 4: A new program appears under my Transfer Approval tab</h3>
<p>When an institution cannot accommodate you in your primary choice due to capacity limits, they may recommend a course change. You must review and accept the transfer on CAPS before processing can continue.</p>

<h3>Scenario 5: I uploaded my O'Level result today—when will my status update?</h3>
<p>Result updates processed at an accredited CBT center usually reflect on the eFacility platform within 24 to 72 hours.</p>

<section id="troubleshooting-delays-action-steps-for-candidates">
<h2>Troubleshooting Delays: Action Steps for Candidates</h2>

<h3>Verifying SSCE Results on the eFacility Portal</h3>
<ol>
<li>Log into your account on the <a href="https://efacility.jamb.gov.ng">JAMB eFacility Portal</a>.</li>
<li>Click on <strong>My O'Level</strong> in the CAPS side navigation bar.</li>
<li>Confirm that all grades (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB) display clearly.</li>
<li>If the page shows "Awaiting Result," visit an accredited CBT center immediately to upload your statement of results.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Reviewing Course Transfer Recommendations</h3>
<ol>
<li>Open the CAPS navigation menu.</li>
<li>Click on the <strong>Transfer Approval</strong> tab.</li>
<li>Check if your institution proposed an alternative course.</li>
<li>If you agree with the new program, click <strong>Accept</strong>. Your main status will then proceed through final validation.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Contacting Institutional Admissions Offices</h3>
<p>If your SSCE results are fully verified, no course transfers are pending, and your status remains stuck on AIP for over three weeks, contact your institution. Reach out to their admissions office or ICT helpdesk with your JAMB Registration Number and Post-UTME details.</p>

<section id="pro-tips-for-navigating-the-caps-process">
<h2>Pro Tips for Navigating the CAPS Process</h2>

<h3>Safeguard Profile Details and Avoid Scams</h3>
<p>Never share your eFacility login credentials or profile code with unverified individuals. Beware of cybercafe operators or social media contacts who claim they can artificially switch your status to "Admitted." Only authorized institutional officials and system automation can update status fields.</p>

<h3>Prepare Administrative Credentials in Advance</h3>
<p>While waiting for your status to update to "Admitted," organize physical copies of your essential documents:</p>
<ul>
<li>Original UTME result slip</li>
<li>O'Level statement of results or certificates</li>
<li>Birth certificate or official declaration of age</li>
<li>Local Government Certificate of Origin</li>
<li>Secondary school testimonial</li>
</ul>

<h3>Monitor Marketplace Opportunities and Backup Choices</h3>
<p>If your admission status does not clear before institutional quotas close, explore the CAPS Marketplace feature. The Marketplace allows other institutions with open vacancies to request your profile for admission consideration.</p>

<section id="frequently-asked-questions-faq">
<h2>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>

<h3>Is Admission in Progress a guarantee of admission?</h3>
<p>No, it is not a final guarantee. It indicates that your chosen school recommended you, but final clearance depends on verification checks by JAMB.</p>

<h3>How many days does Admission in Progress last?</h3>
<p>It typically lasts between 3 and 14 working days, though high-volume institutions may take longer during peak admission periods.</p>

<h3>Can I be rejected after Admission in Progress?</h3>
<p>Yes. If compliance checks reveal invalid subject combinations, age rule violations, or unverified O'Level grades, your status can revert to "Not Admitted."</p>

<h3>Should I visit my school if Admission in Progress takes too long?</h3>
<p>Visiting the institution's admissions desk is reasonable if your status remains stalled for more than three weeks while candidates in your department are already receiving final offers.</p>

<h3>Can I upload my O'Level result while my status is Admission in Progress?</h3>
<p>Yes. If your SSCE results are missing from the portal, visit an accredited CBT center immediately to complete the upload.</p>

<h3>Does Admission in Progress mean my admission letter is ready?</h3>
<p>No. Official Admission Letters can only be generated after your status switches to "Admitted" and you accept the offer on CAPS.</p>
