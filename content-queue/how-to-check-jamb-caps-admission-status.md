<section id="the-dual-portal-admission-workflow">
<h2>The Dual-Portal Admission Workflow</h2>
<p>Admission approval in Nigeria operates on a two-tier verification structure. The university or polytechnic first screens your credentials against departmental cutoff marks. Once cleared locally, your profile moves to the central board for final review.</p>

</section>

<section id="step-by-step-guide-how-to-check-jamb-caps">
<h2>Step-by-Step Guide: How to Check JAMB CAPS</h2>
<p>Checking your status on a smartphone often presents display glitches if the layout defaults to mobile view. Follow these steps to navigate the platform without errors.</p>

<h3>Step 1: Open the e-Facility Portal</h3>
<p>Launch your mobile or desktop web browser and visit <code>efacility.jamb.gov.ng</code>. Enter the email address and password connected to your UTME registration.</p>

<h3>Step 2: Toggle Desktop Mode (Crucial for Mobile Users)</h3>
<p>On smartphones, mobile rendering hides navigation options on the CAPS main menu.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Google Chrome:</strong> Tap the menu icon (three dots, top right) and select <strong>Desktop site</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Safari (iOS):</strong> Tap the <strong>aA</strong> icon in the address bar and tap <strong>Request Desktop Website</strong>.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Step 3: Access the CAPS Interface</h3>
<ol>
<li>Scroll through your e-Facility dashboard.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Check Admission Status</strong>.</li>
<li>Select your examination year (e.g., 2026).</li>
<li>Enter your 10-digit JAMB Registration Number.</li>
<li>Click <strong>Access my CAPS</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Step 4: Locate the Admission Tab</h3>
<p>In the left sidebar menu, select <strong>Admission Status</strong>. Your profile details, course choice, institutional placement, and status message will load on screen.</p>
</section>

<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
<strong>What You Should Do Next</strong>
<ul>
<li>If your status displays <strong>ADMISSION OFFERED</strong>, confirm that your offered course matches your expectations before tapping <strong>ACCEPT</strong>.</li>
<li>If your status displays <strong>NOT ADMITTED</strong>, check the <strong>My O'Level</strong> tab immediately to confirm your SSCE grades are uploaded properly.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<section id="decoding-admission-status-messages">
<h2>Decoding Admission Status Messages</h2>
<p>Admission boards use specific status phrasing to indicate progress. Terminology can vary slightly between admission cycles, but the underlying meanings remain consistent.</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Admission Not Yet Available / Not Admitted:</strong> Your chosen school has not forwarded your name, or your screening score fell below the department cutoff. It also appears if your O'Level uploading process remains incomplete.</li>
<li><strong>Awaiting Recommendation:</strong> Your Post-UTME screening score meets requirements, but the school admission committee has not finished processing its recommendation batch.</li>
<li><strong>Admission in Progress (AIP):</strong> The institution evaluated your results and recommended your candidate profile. The list is currently undergoing administrative verification by JAMB desk officers.</li>
<li><strong>Proposed / Recommended:</strong> You passed both institutional screening and preliminary JAMB automated filters. Formal authorization is pending final sign-off.</li>
<li><strong>Admission Offered:</strong> Admission is officially granted. The <strong>ACCEPT</strong> and <strong>REJECT</strong> action buttons are now clickable.</li>
<li><strong>Transfer Approval:</strong> The institution filled its quota for your original choice and offered you a related program. You must visit the <strong>Transfer Approval</strong> tab to accept the course change before your status updates to Admitted.</li>
<li><strong>Change of Course Pending:</strong> You requested a program edit on the portal, and the application is awaiting approval from both the university registrar and JAMB.</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section id="navigating-school-specific-portals">
<h2>Navigating School-Specific Portals</h2>
<p>While JAMB CAPS provides legal authorization, school portals handle registration, document verification, and fee collection.</p>

<li><strong>Screening Portal:</strong> <code>services.lasu.edu.ng/admissions</code></li>
<li><strong>Verification Method:</strong> Log in using your JAMB registration number and surname. LASU publishes batch lists online while simultaneously transmitting data to CAPS.</li>
</ul>

<h3>2. University of Calabar (UNICAL)</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>Official Web Domain:</strong> <code>unical.edu.ng</code></li>
<li><strong>Admissions Portal:</strong> <code>myunical.edu.ng</code></li>
<li><strong>Verification Method:</strong> Access the portal using your UTME registration number and an e-tranzact pin or transaction ID. Candidates must check both departmental lists and CAPS within 72 hours of publication.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Ebonyi State University (EBSU)</h3>

<li><strong>Admissions Portal:</strong> <code>portal.ebsu.edu.ng</code></li>
<li><strong>Verification Method:</strong> Candidates log in to search merit and supplementary lists, generate acceptance fee invoices, and download clearance documents.</li>

<h3>4. Isa Mustapha Agwai Polytechnic (IMAP, Formerly NASPOLY), Lafia</h3>
<ul>

<section id="comprehensive-troubleshooting-guide">
<h2>Comprehensive Troubleshooting Guide</h2>
<p>Technical glitches during admission windows can cause distress. Here are clear solutions for common issues.</p>

<h3>Issue 1: "Upstream Server Error", "502 Bad Gateway", or Connection Refused</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> This usually indicates a temporary server-side issue caused by heavy traffic, scheduled maintenance, or network congestion.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Avoid continuously clicking refresh. Wait 15 to 30 minutes, or log in during off-peak hours (between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM). Switching internet service providers or clearing browser cache also helps resolve access hiccups.</p>

<h3>Issue 2: Blank Screen on Mobile Browsers</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> On some mobile browsers, the CAPS interface may not display correctly unless Desktop Site is enabled.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Turn on <strong>Desktop site</strong> in your browser settings menu. Switch from portrait to landscape mode if navigation links remain off-screen.</p>

<h3>Issue 3: Accept / Reject Buttons Not Showing or Inactive</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> The buttons remain hidden if your page renders in basic mobile view, or if your status is still at <strong>Admission in Progress (AIP)</strong> rather than <strong>Admission Offered</strong>.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Switch to desktop mode or log in on a computer. If your status says AIP, wait until it formally transitions to <strong>Admission Offered</strong>.</p>

<h3>Issue 4: School Portal Says Admitted, But CAPS Says Not Admitted</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> Institutions upload names locally before or while forwarding recommended lists to JAMB for final approval.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Be patient. The transfer process takes anywhere from a few days to two weeks. Keep checking CAPS daily until your recommendation is processed.</p>

<h3>Issue 5: CAPS Says Admitted, But School Portal Shows Not Admitted</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> JAMB approved your admission directly (often via CAPS Marketplace or supplementary list processing), but the school's ICT department has not updated its local database.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Accept the offer on CAPS first. Print your JAMB Admission Letter and present it to your institution's admissions unit during physical clearance.</p>

<h3>Issue 6: Incorrect O'Level Details or Missing Upload</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> O'Level results uploaded during initial UTME registration were corrupted or missing key subject grades.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Visit an accredited JAMB CBT center. Request a fresh O'Level upload directly onto your JAMB profile. Institutions cannot process candidates with pending O'Level status on CAPS.</p>

<h3>Issue 7: Forgotten Password or Wrong Email Linked</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> Candidates lose access to their profile email or forget password credentials created during UTME registration.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Use the password reset link on <code>efacility.jamb.gov.ng</code>. If your email is inaccessible or was incorrectly entered, visit a JAMB office or accredited CBT center for biometric email linking and profile corrections.</p>

<h3>Issue 8: Payment Verification Issues or Session Expired Errors</h3>
<p><strong>Explanation:</strong> Network timed out during fee transaction processing on the school portal.</p>
<p><strong>Fix:</strong> Do not attempt a second payment immediately. Log out, clear browser cookies, and wait 24 hours for interbank settlement systems to reconcile the transaction. Check your bank account for auto-reversals before retrying.</p>
</section>

<section id="frequently-asked-questions-faqs">
<h2>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</h2>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I check JAMB CAPS on my phone?</summary>
<p>Yes. You can check CAPS on a mobile device by opening Google Chrome or Safari and enabling <strong>Desktop site</strong> view in your browser options menu.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How often should I check my portal during admission season?</summary>
<p>Check your status at least twice a week once your school releases its screening results. Increase frequency to daily when target lists drop.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I reject an admission offer on CAPS?</summary>
<p>Yes. If you decline an offer, your profile returns to the national pool, making you eligible for consideration by other institutions through CAPS Marketplace or supplementary processing.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I accept an offer and still change institutions later?</summary>
<p>No. Once you tap <strong>ACCEPT</strong> on CAPS, your admission is locked for that academic session. JAMB will block other institutions from offering you alternative placement.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What happens if I fail to accept my admission before the deadline?</summary>
<p>Failure to accept an offer within the stipulated window (often 2 to 4 weeks depending on institutional policy) can lead to offer revocation. Unaccepted seats are reassigned to supplementary candidates.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Why does my school say admitted while CAPS shows "Not Admitted"?</summary>
<p>This delay happens while recommendations move through administrative reviews between university registrars and JAMB desk officers. Your CAPS status will update once verification finishes.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How long does "Admission in Progress" (AIP) take?</summary>
<p>AIP status typically lasts between a few days to three weeks, depending on how quickly JAMB processes institutional batch lists.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I upload O'Level results after Post-UTME screening?</summary>
<p>Yes. You can upload awaiting results at any accredited JAMB CBT center even after completing Post-UTME, provided the school has not closed its admission session.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Is accepting admission on CAPS enough to secure my seat?</summary>
<p>No. CAPS acceptance provides legal validation, but you must also pay your school's non-refundable acceptance fee and complete physical screening to secure your enrollment.</p>
</details>
</section>
