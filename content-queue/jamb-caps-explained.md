<section id="what-is-caps">
  <h2>What is JAMB CAPS?</h2>
  <blockquote class="def">
    <b>JAMB CAPS</b> (Central Admissions Processing System) is the automated, web-based platform the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board uses to coordinate admissions into Nigerian tertiary institutions. It prevents duplicate offers and is the sole legally recognized system for accepting or rejecting admission.
  </blockquote>
  <p>Before CAPS, admissions ran on decentralized physical lists — a process prone to delays and situations where one candidate held multiple slots while other qualified applicants had none. CAPS was introduced under Registrar Prof. Is-haq Oloyede to route every offer through one central database, applying merit, catchment area, and Educationally Less Developed States (ELDS) guidelines consistently. No university, polytechnic, or college of education can issue a legally recognized admission outside this system.</p>
</section>

<section id="dashboard">
  <h2>Navigating your candidate dashboard</h2>
  <p>Your registered email must be linked to your JAMB profile before you can access CAPS. Learn more in our guide on <a href="/posts/how-to-check-jamb-caps-admission-status">how to check JAMB CAPS admission status</a> step by step. Once linked:</p>
  <ol class="steps">
    <li>Open a browser and go to the <a href="https://efacility.jamb.gov.ng" target="_blank" rel="noopener">JAMB e-Facility Portal</a>.</li>
    <li>Sign in with your registered email address and password.</li>
    <li>Select <b>Check Admission Status</b>.</li>
    <li>Choose the current examination year and enter your registration number.</li>
    <li>Select <b>Access my CAPS</b> to open the dashboard.</li>
  </ol>
  <div class="callout tip">
    <strong class="label">MOBILE FIX</strong>
    If the dashboard menu doesn't render fully on a phone, open your browser's settings and enable <b>Desktop site</b> (three-dot menu in Chrome). This is the most common reason candidates report a blank or broken Accept button on mobile.
  </div>

    <thead><tr><th>Tab</th><th>What it does</th></tr></thead>

<section id="status">
  <h2>Decoding your admission status</h2>
  <p>Status updates automatically as the cycle progresses. Here is the real order candidates move through during the screening window:</p>
  <div class="status-rail">
    <div class="status-step">
      <span class="tag">Default</span>
      <h4>Not Admitted / No Offer Yet</h4>
      <p>Your school hasn't uploaded its current recommendation batch, or your profile is still being screened. Read <a href="/posts/what-to-do-if-jamb-caps-says-not-admitted">what to do if JAMB CAPS says Not Admitted</a> for next steps.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="status-step">
      <span class="tag">In progress</span>
      <h4>Admission in Progress (AIP)</h4>
      <p>The school has forwarded your name to JAMB's registry. Strong positive signal, but not final until JAMB desk officers verify your subject combination and O'Level credits. See our full breakdown on <a href="/posts/admission-in-progress-status-meaning">Admission in Progress status meaning</a>.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="status-step">
      <span class="tag">Backend</span>
      <h4>Approved</h4>
      <p>JAMB has cleared the school's recommendation. Usually converts to "Admitted" within a few days.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="status-step final">
      <span class="tag">Final</span>
      <h4>Admitted</h4>
      <p>Official confirmation. The Accept / Reject buttons go live on your dashboard.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="status-step negative">
      <span class="tag">Reversed</span>
      <h4>Withdrawn / Cancelled</h4>
      <p>An institution pulled back its recommendation — often due to result discrepancies, age eligibility, or a change in available capacity.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="status-step negative">
      <span class="tag">Reversed</span>
      <h4>Failed Screening</h4>
      <p>You didn't meet the post-UTME cut-off, or credential verification failed.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="marketplace">
  <h2>The CAPS Marketplace: a second opportunity</h2>
  <blockquote class="def">
    The <b>CAPS Marketplace</b> is a digital forum where institutions with vacant slots can find and recruit high-scoring UTME candidates who missed their first-choice cut-off due to competitive quotas.
  </blockquote>
  <p>If you score well but miss a highly competitive cut-off (Medicine, Law), schools with open slots in related fields can find you through the Marketplace and make an offer. You must actively accept a Marketplace offer on your dashboard — doing so doesn't automatically cancel your standing with your original choices unless you go on to accept a final status change.</p>
</section>

<section id="accept">
  <h2>Accepting or rejecting your offer</h2>
  <div class="callout warn">
    <strong class="label">THE 4-WEEK RULE — RECONFIRMED FOR 2026</strong>
    JAMB's Annual Policy Meeting formalized a four-week grace period for candidates to accept or reject an approved offer. Miss the window, and the institution can request its removal — the slot is recycled to another candidate.
  </div>
  <h3>Option A — the portal</h3>
  <p>For a complete detailed breakdown, check our dedicated guide on <a href="/posts/how-to-accept-admission-on-jamb-caps">how to accept admission on JAMB CAPS</a>.</p>
  <ol class="steps">
    <li>Open the <b>Admission Status</b> tab on CAPS.</li>
    <li>Review the institution, program, and registration details shown.</li>
    <li>Click <b>Accept Admission</b>.</li>
    <li>Confirm on the pop-up. Your status updates to "Admission Accepted."</li>
  </ol>
  <h3>Option B — SMS (no internet access)</h3>
  <p>Using the SIM linked to your JAMB profile:</p>
  <ul>
    <li>Check status: send <b>STATUS 2026</b> to <b>55019</b> or <b>66019</b>.</li>
    <li>Accept or reject: send <b>ACCEPT</b> or <b>REJECT</b> followed by your registration number to <b>55019</b> or <b>66019</b>. Standard network fees may apply.</li>
  </ul>
  <h3>The waiting dilemma</h3>
  <p>Once you accept, your profile locks to that institution — no other school can offer you a spot. If you're holding out for a first-choice offer while a second-choice offer sits on your dashboard, weigh the two clocks above: your personal four weeks, and that institution's national deadline. Waiting too long risks losing the offer you already have.</p>
</section>

<section id="trouble">
  <h2>Troubleshooting common issues</h2>
  <h3>O'Level uploads missing</h3>
  <p>If your UTME sitting shows "Awaiting Result," your final grades must be uploaded at an accredited CBT centre. See our guide on <a href="/posts/how-to-upload-olevel-result-jamb-portal">how to upload O'Level results on JAMB portal</a>. Check the <b>My O'Level Result</b> tab to confirm — institutions bypass files where this is empty.</p>
  <h3>School says "Admitted," CAPS says otherwise</h3>
  <p>Schools process selections locally before uploading to JAMB's central database. Give it up to two weeks to synchronize. If it doesn't update, visit the institution's admissions office to confirm your recommendation was actually forwarded.</p>
  <h3>Data corrections</h3>
  <p>If you need to change your choice of course or institution, check our guide on submitting a <a href="/posts/jamb-change-of-course-form">JAMB change of course form</a>.</p>
  <ol class="steps">
    <li>Log in to the e-Facility Portal.</li>
    <li>Select <b>Correction of Data</b> from the sidebar.</li>
    <li>Pay the applicable fee online (confirm current amounts on the portal).</li>
    <li>Complete biometric verification at an accredited CBT centre or JAMB office to finalize.</li>
  </ol>
</section>

<section id="faq">
  <h2>Frequently asked questions</h2>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Can I reject an offer after clicking Accept?</summary>
    <p>No. Once accepted on CAPS, the decision is recorded in the central registry and cannot be reversed for the current session.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Does checking my status often affect my application?</summary>
    <p>No. Check as often as you like — regular monitoring just helps you avoid missing your four-week window.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Is there a cost to use CAPS?</summary>
    <p>Checking your admission status is free. Printing your official admission letter requires an online payment.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Why does CAPS show "No Match Found"?</summary>
    <p>Usually the wrong examination year was selected at login, or there's a mismatch between your registration profile and linked email. Verify your details and try again.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Who is currently the JAMB Registrar?</summary>
    <p>Prof. Is-haq Oloyede's tenure as Registrar ended on July 31, 2026, after two five-year terms marked by the introduction of CAPS itself. His successor, Prof. Segun Aina, a Professor of Computer Engineering at Obafemi Awolowo University, took over as Registrar from that date.</p>
  </details>
</section>

<section id="action-plan">
  <div class="action-plan">
    <h3>Your action plan</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>Verify O'Level uploads</b> — check early; visit a CBT centre if the tab is empty.</li>
      <li><b>Confirm subject combinations</b> against your institution's requirements; file a change-of-course request if mismatched.</li>
      <li><b>Check CAPS weekly</b> for AIP or status changes.</li>
      <li><b>Accept within 4 weeks</b> of "Admitted" — and don't wait until your institution's national deadline is close.</li>
      <li><b>Print your admission letter</b> once accepted, to complete clearance at your institution.</li>
    </ul>
