<p>Did your UTME score miss your dream course's departmental cut-off mark? Or did you register with a conflicting O'level subject combination? The JAMB data correction window provides a second chance. This guide maps out how to modify your choices safely, avoid common online payment traps, and successfully push your new details to your target institution.</p>

<div class="action-plan">
<h3>Key Takeaways</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>The Real Cost:</strong> The base <em>JAMB change of course fee</em> is ₦2,500. However, bank gateway commissions and local CBT administrative fees bring your actual out-of-pocket cost higher.</li>
<li><strong>Biometrics are Mandatory:</strong> You cannot complete a <em>JAMB correction of data</em> entirely on a mobile phone or personal laptop. You must physically visit an accredited CBT center or JAMB office for fingerprint verification.</li>
<li><strong>CAPS Syncing is Dynamic:</strong> A <em>JAMB CAPS update</em> is not always instant. It can take anywhere from a few hours to two weeks for your new choices to reflect.</li>
<li><strong>Subject Compatibility is King:</strong> Changing your course without matching your O'level and UTME subject combinations to the requirements in the <a href="/jamb-brochure-guide">JAMB Brochure Guide</a> will lead to automatic screening disqualification.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<section id="1-understanding-the-change-of-course-and-institution-service">
<h2 id="understanding-the-service">1. Understanding the Change of Course and Institution Service</h2>
<p>Nigerian university admissions are highly competitive. A slight deficit in your UTME score can compromise your prospects of studying highly competitive programs like Medicine or Law at premium federal institutions.</p>

<p>The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board built the data correction portal as an administrative safety valve. This service allows you to swap unrealistic choices for pragmatic alternatives where your scores align with departmental cut-off marks.</p>

<p>Another frequent driver for this correction is a subject combination error. If you sat for Government, Literature, and Economics but applied for Accounting, most institutions will flag you during screening. The change of course service lets you search for programs that accept your actual exam subjects.</p>
</section>

<section id="3-the-process-pathway-timeline">
<h2 id="process-timeline">3. The Process Pathway & Timeline</h2>
<p>The path to updating your choices is straightforward but requires moving between digital platforms and physical spaces. Below is the typical operational order.</p>

<ol class="steps">
<li>Verify subject requirements in the official IBASS database.</li>
<li>Log into JAMB e-Facility & pay the ₦2,500 fee.</li>
<li>Generate and print your unique Transaction ID.</li>
<li>Visit an accredited CBT Centre for biometrics.</li>
<li>Review details on the operator's screen and submit.</li>
<li>Print the official Change of Data Slip.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Dynamic Deadlines</h3>
<p>JAMB does not enforce a single, rigid general deadline for data corrections. Instead, deadlines are dynamic and governed by individual institutional calendars.</p>

<p>Once a university or polytechnic closes its Post-UTME registration portal or submits its final admission list to JAMB CAPS, changes to that specific institution become highly restricted or functionally impossible. For the current admission cycle, candidates must execute all changes before their target school's screening portal closes.</p>
</section>
<section id="4-navigating-the-jamb-e-facility-portal">
<h2 id="navigating-portal">4. Navigating the JAMB e-Facility Portal</h2>
<p>The gateway to all modifications is the official <em>JAMB e-Facility portal</em>. Avoid cloned blogs or social media mirrors.</p>

<ol>
<li>Go directly to the official URL: <a href="https://efacility.jamb.gov.ng/" target="_blank">https://efacility.jamb.gov.ng/</a>.</li>
<li>Input your registered email address and password. (See our <a href="/jamb-profile-creation">JAMB Profile Creation</a> guide if you have issues logging in).</li>
<li>On your user dashboard, locate the left sidebar.</li>
<li>Click on <strong>Correction of Data</strong>.</li>
<li>From the options revealed, select <strong>Course/Institution</strong>.</li>
</ol>

<div class="callout warn">
<strong class="label">IMPORTANT SECURITY NOTE</strong>
Do not attempt to complete the submission steps using a mobile browser. Biometric security measures require specialized hardware found only at accredited centers.
</div>
</section>
<section id="5-step-by-step-guide-how-to-apply">
<h2 id="step-by-step">5. Step-by-Step Guide: How to Apply</h2>
<p>Follow these steps to complete your change during the current admission cycle:</p>

<h3>Step 1: Confirm Course Eligibility</h3>
<p>Before initiating any transaction, check your subject match. Check the <a href="/jamb-brochure-guide">JAMB Brochure Guide</a> (using IBASS). Ensure your O'level results match the requirements of the new course and that your UTME scores meet the likely <a href="/jamb-cut-off-marks">JAMB Cut-Off Marks</a>.</p>

<h3>Step 2: Generate Your Payment on e-Facility</h3>
<p>Log into your profile, navigate to the "Correction of Data" menu, and choose "Course/Institution". Select your applicable exam year. A bold red <strong>Transaction ID</strong> will be generated on your confirmation page. Copy this ID and check your email inbox for a duplicate copy.</p>

<h3>Step 3: Complete Payment</h3>
<p>Choose your payment gateway (typically Remita). You can pay with your ATM debit card immediately or print the RRR code to pay at any physical bank branch.</p>

<h3>Step 4: Visit an Accredited CBT Center</h3>
<p>Once your transaction shows "Approved", take your printed payment slip and JAMB registration number to the nearest accredited CBT center.</p>

<h3>Step 5: Biometric Capture & Choice Updates</h3>
<p>The CBT operator will input your registration details. You will be prompted to place your finger on the biometric scanner. This acts as your digital signature, authorizing the changes. Provide the operator with your new choices.</p>

<h3>Step 6: Print Confirmation Slip</h3>
<p>Confirm that there are no spelling errors on the screen. Click submit. The operator will print out your "JAMB Change of Data Slip". Keep this secure. It is a vital document during your <a href="/post-utme-registration-guide">Post-UTME Registration Guide</a> and physical clearance.</p>
</section>
<section id="6-fees-and-cbt-charges">
<h2 id="fees-breakdown">6. Fees and CBT Charges</h2>
<p>Understanding what is official policy versus typical candidate experience prevents unexpected financial surprises.</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>The Official JAMB Portal Fee:</strong> ₦2,500. This is the mandatory cost of the service across the board, as outlined in the official JAMB service rate sheet.</li>
<li><strong>The Official Service Charge:</strong> JAMB’s official charging table lists a ₦200 service fee for non-JAMB-owned CBT centers executing corrections.</li>
<li><strong>Variable CBT Charges:</strong> Many CBT centers may charge additional administrative fees. These vary by location and should be confirmed directly with the center before payment. Expect your actual out-of-pocket costs to sit around ₦3,000 to ₦3,500 once processing fees, network transaction rates, and printing fees are calculated.</li>
</ul>
</section>
<section id="7-checklist-before-you-visit-a-cbt-centre">
<h2 id="before-you-pay-checklist">7. Checklist: Before You Visit a CBT Centre</h2>
<p>Avoid wasting resources. Go through this checklist step-by-step:</p>

<ul>
<li><input type="checkbox" disabled> <strong>UTME Score Alignment:</strong> Does your score realistically fall near or above the target school's departmental cut-off?</li>
<li><input type="checkbox" disabled> <strong>Subject Combination Check:</strong> Do your four UTME subjects match the new department's guidelines in the JAMB Brochure?</li>
<li><input type="checkbox" disabled> <strong>O'level Requirements Met:</strong> Do you have credit passes in English, Mathematics, and other core subjects required for this new program?</li>
<li><input type="checkbox" disabled> <strong>Verified O'level Upload:</strong> Have you uploaded your O'level results to CAPS? (See our <a href="/olevel-upload-guide">O'Level Upload Guide</a>).</li>
<li><input type="checkbox" disabled> <strong>School's Portal Status:</strong> Is the target school's Post-UTME form still on sale? (Confirm via the university's official admissions website).</li>
<li><input type="checkbox" disabled> <strong>CBT Center Accreditation:</strong> Is the center you are visiting on the official list of accredited centers for this admission cycle?</li>
</ul>
</section>
<section id="8-common-mistakes-candidates-make">
<h2 id="common-mistakes">8. Common Mistakes Candidates Make</h2>
<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID</strong>
<ul>
<li><strong>Blind Transitions:</strong> Changing to a course without double-checking the specific O'level and UTME subject requirements.</li>
<li><strong>Late Action:</strong> Waiting until the target school's Post-UTME portal has completely closed before attempting a change of institution on the JAMB portal.</li>
<li><strong>Using Unofficial Agents:</strong> Handing login details to unverified third parties who claim they can bypass physical biometric scanning.</li>
<li><strong>Skipping O'Level Uploads:</strong> Assuming that a change of course automatically uploads or updates your WAEC/NECO results on CAPS.</li>
<li><strong>Losing the Slip:</strong> Forgetting to collect and safely store the printed Change of Data slip from the CBT center.</li>
<li><strong>Assuming Instant CAPS Syncing:</strong> Expecting the change to reflect on your admission screening portal immediately without waiting for server propagation.</li>
</ul>
</div>
</section>
<section id="9-common-portal-errors-troubleshooting">
<h2 id="common-errors">9. Common Portal Errors & Troubleshooting</h2>

<h3>1. Payment Deducted but "Pending" on e-Facility</h3>
<p><strong>Why it happens:</strong> Payment gateway sync delays between Remita and JAMB's accounting server.<br>
<strong>How to fix:</strong> Go to "My Payment Section" on your dashboard, locate the failed/pending transaction, and click the <strong>Re-Query</strong> button. This forces a manual check and usually updates your status to approved within seconds. Avoid paying a second time immediately.</p>

<h3>2. Target Course or School Missing from Dropdown</h3>
<p><strong>Why it happens:</strong> The school may have officially concluded admissions for that session, or the specific course is full or lacks NUC accreditation for the current academic session.<br>
<strong>How to fix:</strong> Verify the status of the program on the university's official page. If the program is unavailable, you will need to select a different, closely related program.</p>

<h3>3. "Biometric Match Not Found" during Scanning</h3>
<p><strong>Why it happens:</strong> Dirty scanner screens, dry fingers, or local network lag at the CBT center.<br>
<strong>How to fix:</strong> Wash your hands, apply minor moisture, or ask the operator to clean the optical reader. If the mismatch persists, you may need to visit an official JAMB state office to complete the correction.</p>
</section>
<section id="10-impact-on-admission-caps-synchronisation">
<h2 id="caps-impact">10. Impact on Admission & CAPS Synchronisation</h2>

<h3>How Changing Your Choice Affects Admission Prospects</h3>
<p>Making a change of course or school can alter your admission chances. Moving from an oversubscribed course (such as Medicine) to an undersubscribed, related program (such as Physiology) raises your likelihood of being proposed for admission on CAPS. Your admission prospects become much more difficult unless you choose realistic options that fit your UTME scores and subject combinations.</p>

<h3>Understanding JAMB CAPS Update and Syncing Timelines</h3>
<p>The <strong>JAMB change of institution processing time</strong> on CAPS varies. Under typical conditions, your new selection should update on CAPS within 24 to 72 hours. However, during high-volume periods, data migration might take up to two weeks. You can monitor this progress by accessing your "Admission Status" page on your CAPS mobile view or desktop portal.</p>
</section>
<section id="11-frequently-asked-questions-faqs">
<h2 id="faqs">11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</h2>

<h3>How many times can I change institution?</h3>
<p>JAMB permits multiple changes subject to prevailing policies and payment of the applicable fee for each request. However, some individual tertiary institutions do not process late adjustments, so it is highly recommended to finalize your choices early.</p>

<h3>Does changing course affect admission?</h3>
<p>Yes, dramatically. It does not change your raw UTME score, but it places your application in a completely different pool of competitors. If you move to a course with lower cut-off marks, your admission prospects generally rise.</p>

<h3>Can I change institution after Post-UTME?</h3>
<p>Yes, but with heavy caveats. If the new school’s Post-UTME screening window is still open or they accept late applications, you can apply. However, if the target institution has already compiled their primary admission list, they will not see your details, making your change ineffective.</p>

<h3>Can I change institution after accepting admission?</h3>
<p>Generally, no. Once you click "Accept" on your CAPS dashboard, your registration is tied to that institution. See our <a href="/how-to-accept-admission-on-caps">How to Accept Admission on CAPS</a> guide for details. Bypassing this after acceptance requires an institutional program transfer or indemnity waiver, which is subject to strict guidelines.</p>

<h3>Can I revert a change of course?</h3>
<p>Yes, you can change your preferences back to your original choices. However, this is treated as a completely new transaction. You will need to pay the <em>JAMB change of course fee</em> again and repeat the biometric process at a CBT center.</p>
</section>
<section id="12-alternative-paths-crucial-warnings">
<h2 id="alternative-paths">12. Alternative Paths & Crucial Warnings</h2>

<h3>Avoid Unaccredited Cyber Cafes</h3>
<p>Many desperate candidates fall victim to roadside operators who claim they can update choices "remotely" without biometrics. These claims are false.</p>

<p>JAMB's portal architecture requires physical thumbprinting for final submission. Anyone claiming to execute this online without your presence is either using an unauthorized exploit (which risks profile blacklisting) or simply defrauding you. Always protect your personal <a href="/jamb-profile-creation">JAMB Profile Creation</a> login details.</p>
</section>
