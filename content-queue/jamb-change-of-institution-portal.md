<p>Receiving your UTME results marks a clear turning point in your higher education journey. When an aggregate score lands below the historical entry bar for your target university or program, your initial strategy must adapt. The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board provides an official data correction mechanism to help candidates realign their choices.</p>

<p>Switching your choice of institution or course is a practical realignment strategy. Thousands of Nigerian candidates adjust their academic destinations every session, transferring their applications to institutions where their scores align better with departmental cut-off requirements.</p>

<section id="understanding-the-jamb-change-of-course-institution-process">
<h2>Understanding the JAMB Change of Course & Institution Process</h2>

<p>The JAMB change of course and institution feature functions as an administrative adjustment window. After UTME results are published, tertiary institutions across Nigeria publish their departmental cut-off benchmarks and post-UTME screening parameters. The change portal allows you to align your choices with these published criteria before institutional registration portals close.</p>

<h3>Why Would You Need to Change Your Institution or Course?</h3>

<p>Candidates apply for adjustments through the portal for several distinct reasons:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Aligning with Departmental Cut-off Marks:</strong> Aggregate scores dictate your competitiveness. If you scored 195 and your initial choice sets its baseline at 230, staying on that list lowers your chances. Transferring to an institution with a threshold matching your performance creates a more realistic path forward.</li>
<li><strong>Shifting Academic Interests:</strong> Career goals can evolve between original registration and the post-exam phase. You might choose to switch from Computer Science to Information Technology, or shift from Medicine to Medical Laboratory Science.</li>
<li><strong>Program Accreditation & Availability:</strong> Institutional program offerings change. A university may temporarily pause intake for specific courses due to regulatory or accreditation reviews, requiring candidates to pick alternative programs.</li>
<li><strong>Institutional First-Choice Policies:</strong> Several federal and state universities exclusively consider candidates who select them as their First Choice. If your original first choice drops your intended course, updating your choices becomes necessary.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Key Definitions: Course vs. Institution vs. Data Correction</h3>

<p>Understanding these distinctions prevents costly errors during administrative processing:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Change of Institution:</strong> Replacing the university, polytechnic, or college of education listed on your profile. You can modify your 1st, 2nd, 3rd, or 4th choice selections.</li>
<li><strong>Change of Course:</strong> Modifying your intended major within your existing institution or inside a newly selected school.</li>
<li><strong>Data Correction:</strong> An umbrella term on the JAMB e-Facility portal that includes modifications to names, dates of birth, gender, state/LGA of origin, course, or institutional choices.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Timeline & Deadlines for the 2026/2027 Cycle</h3>

<p>Timing dictates success during the admission season. The JAMB change portal generally opens shortly after UTME results drop and remains open throughout the central exercise. However, candidate deadlines are dictated by individual post-UTME windows set by tertiary institutions.</p>

<p>If a state university sets its post-UTME deadline for late August, your institutional update must be completed and reflected on the central database before that registration window closes. Relying on national portal availability alone is risky; you must track your specific target school’s timetable.</p>

<h3>Official Policy Framework & Source References</h3>

<p>According to official regulations published in the <a href="https://portal.jamb.gov.ng/efacility" target="_blank" rel="noopener">JAMB e-Facility Portal</a> guidelines and the official JAMB Bulletin, candidates may request modifications provided they complete official payment for each session. JAMB policy mandates that all changes must strictly comply with course subject combinations published in the <a href="https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng" target="_blank" rel="noopener">JAMB iBASS Portal</a>. Furthermore, security mandates require biometric thumbprint authentication at an accredited CBT center to validate institutional updates.</p>
</section>
<section id="eligibility-criteria-can-you-make-a-change">
<h2>Eligibility Criteria: Can You Make a Change?</h2>

<p>Not all candidate profiles are eligible for data modifications. Structural rules dictate who can use the portal.</p>

<h3>Who is Eligible?</h3>

<p>You qualify to process a change if you meet the following conditions:</p>
<ul>
<li>You sat for the 2026 UTME or submitted an application through Direct Entry (DE) for the 2026/2027 cycle.</li>
<li>You hold a valid 10-digit JAMB profile code connected to an active phone number and email address.</li>
<li>You have not formally accepted an admission offer on JAMB CAPS for the current cycle.</li>
<li>Your UTME subject combination satisfies the minimum prerequisites for your newly selected major.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Who is NOT Eligible?</h3>

<p>Your profile will be restricted from processing changes under these specific circumstances:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Accepted Admission Status:</strong> Accepting an offer on JAMB CAPS generally restricts further automated transfers, as JAMB treats an accepted seat as a finalized placement for that academic cycle.</li>
<li><strong>Subject Mismatches:</strong> If your UTME subject combination fails to match the compulsory course requirements listed in the official brochure, the system will reject the combination.</li>
<li><strong>Account Flags:</strong> Profiles flagged for investigation or examination misconduct are restricted from accessing data correction services.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Direct Entry (DE) Applicants: Specific Rules & Requirements</h3>

<p>Direct Entry candidates follow specific guidelines when navigating institutional updates:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Qualification Mapping:</strong> DE candidates must verify that their advanced level qualification (JUPEB, IJMB, NCE, ND, HND, or First Degree) is accepted by the newly selected institution.</li>
<li><strong>Verification Delays:</strong> Changing institutions as a DE candidate requires the new school to verify your A'Level transcript. Factor this extra processing time into your application plan.</li>
<li><strong>Direct Entry Course Alignment:</strong> DE updates must align with your background discipline. Switching from an ND in Building Technology to a Direct Entry Law program is typically blocked by faculty rules.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Required Documents and Information Checklist</h3>

<p>Before visiting an accredited registration center, prepare these required items:</p>
<ol>
<li>10-digit JAMB Registration Number.</li>
<li>Official JAMB Profile Code.</li>
<li>Portal Login Details (Email address and password for the e-Facility portal).</li>
<li>O'Level Result Details (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB statement of results or certificates).</li>
<li>National Identification Number (NIN).</li>
<li>Target institution details and exact departmental options.</li>
<li>Payment method or cash for center administrative charges.</li>
</ol>

<section id="step-by-step-guide-navigating-the-jamb-portal">
<h2>Step-by-Step Guide: Navigating the JAMB Portal</h2>

<p>Completing a course or institutional modification requires online payment generation followed by physical biometric verification.</p>

<h3>Accessing the Official JAMB Portal</h3>

<p>Navigate directly to the official portal domain at <code>portal.jamb.gov.ng/efacility</code>. Avoid unverified third-party portals or short links. Log in using your verified email address and account password.</p>

<h3>Locating the "Correction of Data" Section</h3>

<p>Once on your dashboard:</p>
<ol>
<li>Locate the left navigation menu.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Correction of Data</strong>.</li>
<li>Click on <strong>Course/Institution</strong>.</li>
<li>Select the current examination year (<strong>2026</strong>).</li>
<li>The portal will generate a unique <strong>Transaction ID</strong>. Copy and save this reference number immediately.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Filling Out the Application Form</h3>

<p>The portal interface displays your current registered choices alongside input fields for updates.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Personal Details Verification:</strong> Check that your registration number and full names match your original UTME documentation.</li>
<li><strong>Selecting New Institutions:</strong> Select your preferred category (Federal University, State University, Polytechnic, Monotechnic, or College of Education). Choose the specific school and your updated program.</li>
<li><strong>Document Uploads:</strong> If you are updating O'Level records alongside your application, input accurate grade breakdowns. Uploaded images must stay within standard size limits (generally under 100KB in JPG or PNG format).</li>
</ul>

<h3>Payment Process & Fee Breakdown</h3>

<p>Charges follow a standard national structure, though local center administrative fees vary slightly.</p>

<p>Payment can be completed online via Remita using debit cards, internet banking, or USSD channels. Once processed, your dashboard status will display as "PAID".</p>

<h3>Completing Application Submission via Biometrics</h3>

<p>Generating a transaction slip on your phone does <strong>not</strong> complete the change. You must visit a JAMB-accredited CBT center or a state JAMB office.</p>

<p>The center operator logs into the administrative terminal using your Transaction ID, enters your choice selections, and prompts you for biometric fingerprint verification. Once your identity is confirmed by the scanner, the operator submits the request and issues an official JAMB Data Correction Slip. Keep this stamped document for physical screening.</p>
</section>
<section id="common-mistakes-to-avoid">
<h2>Common Mistakes to Avoid</h2>

<p>A single administrative oversight can delay your application process. Watch out for these frequent mistakes:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Ignoring UTME Subject Prerequisites:</strong> Changing to a course without writing the required UTME subject combination leads to automatic rejection on CAPS.</li>
<li><strong>Missing Post-UTME Deadlines:</strong> Initiating a portal change after your target institution has closed its Post-UTME portal renders the transfer useless for that session.</li>
<li><strong>Selecting Restricted Choice Categories:</strong> Picking institutions as 2nd or 3rd choices when those specific schools explicitly demand 1st Choice placement on their admissions portal.</li>
<li><strong>Selecting Unaccredited Programs:</strong> Failing to verify program status on iBASS before applying.</li>
<li><strong>Forgetting O'Level Uploads:</strong> Changing your institution without verifying that your O'Level grades are uploaded on CAPS stops the new school from processing your application. Refer to our O'Level Result Upload Guide for step-by-step instructions.</li>
</ol>
</section>
<section id="the-role-of-jamb-caps-in-your-change-request">
<h2>The Role of JAMB CAPS in Your Change Request</h2>

<p>The Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) serves as the primary engine managing institutional recommendations and JAMB approvals.</p>

<h3>What is JAMB CAPS and Why is it Important?</h3>

<p>JAMB CAPS provides a transparent framework for managing tertiary admissions. Every update processed at a CBT center must sync to the CAPS engine before your chosen institution can view your profile or recommend you for admission.</p>

<h3>How to Check Your Change Status on CAPS</h3>

<ol>
<li>Log into <code>portal.jamb.gov.ng/efacility</code>.</li>
<li>Enable <strong>Desktop Site</strong> mode if using a mobile browser.</li>
<li>Click on <strong>Check Admission Status</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Access my CAPS</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>My Choices</strong> on the sidebar to check if your updated institution and course appear.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Understanding CAPS Statuses</h3>

<p>Status updates reflect real-time progress between the university and JAMB:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Pending Approval:</strong> Your profile details are accessible to the new institution, but formal evaluation is ongoing.</li>
<li><strong>Transfer Approval:</strong> The school proposes placing you in a different department based on your score profile.</li>
<li><strong>Recommended:</strong> The institution's admission board has submitted your application to JAMB for final approval.</li>
<li><strong>Approved / Admission Offered:</strong> JAMB and the university have officially confirmed your seat.</li>
<li><strong>Rejected:</strong> Your profile did not meet the department's final selection parameters.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Next Steps After CAPS Approval or Rejection</h3>

<ul>
<li><strong>If Approved:</strong> Click <strong>Accept Admission</strong> to secure your placement. Avoid clicking "Reject" unless you plan to decline the offer entirely. Print your official Admission Letter and Result Slip.</li>
<li><strong>If Rejected:</strong> Check for alternative programs with open vacancies, or explore the <strong>CAPS Marketplace</strong> tab to review direct admission requests from other institutions.</li>
</ul>
</section>
<section id="troubleshooting-common-errors-expanded-faqs">
<h2>Troubleshooting Common Errors & Expanded FAQs</h2>

<p>Technical issues can occur during data modification. Here is how to resolve them.</p>

<h3>Resolving Portal Errors & Glitches</h3>

<p>Network timeouts usually happen during peak screening periods. If you face system errors, clear your browser cache, switch to an incognito window, or access the portal during low-traffic periods between 11:00 PM and 5:00 AM.</p>

<h3>Payment Reconciliation</h3>

<p>If funds are deducted from your account via Remita but the portal status remains "Unpaid":</p>
<ol>
<li>Log into your dashboard at <code>portal.jamb.gov.ng/efacility</code>.</li>
<li>Navigate to <strong>My Payment History</strong>.</li>
<li>Select your Transaction ID and click <strong>Reconcile Payment</strong>.</li>
<li>The system will query the payment gateway and update your dashboard.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h3>

<p><strong>Can I change my institution or course more than once?</strong><br>
Yes. JAMB permits multiple data modifications, provided you complete the payment process for each separate transaction. However, institutional registration deadlines limit your window of opportunity.</p>

<p><strong>Can I change both my course and institution in the same application?</strong><br>
Yes. The ₦2,500 portal fee covers changes to both your course and institution options during a single application session at the CBT terminal.</p>

<p><strong>Will my original 2026 UTME score change after processing a correction?</strong><br>
No. Your score remains completely unchanged. Data corrections only modify your choice parameters, not your examination results.</p>

<p><strong>Does changing institution affect my admission chances?</strong><br>
Relocating your choice to an institution where your UTME score exceeds the historical baseline can improve your selection likelihood, depending on applicant volume and departmental quotas.</p>

<p><strong>Can I revert to my previous institution if I change my mind?</strong><br>
You can return to a previous choice only by initiating a fresh change application, paying the required fee, and completing biometric verification again at a CBT center before registration deadlines pass.</p>

<p><strong>Can I complete the entire process on a mobile phone?</strong><br>
You can generate payment references and monitor CAPS status on a phone, but physical biometric thumbprint validation at an accredited CBT center is strictly required to finalize the process.</p>

<p><strong>How long does it take for changes to reflect on CAPS?</strong><br>
Updates typically sync across JAMB CAPS and institutional portals within 24 hours to 7 days after biometric submission.</p>
</section>
<section id="after-the-change-next-steps">
<h2>After the Change: Next Steps</h2>

<p>Submitting your form at the CBT terminal is the first phase of transferring your application.</p>

<h3>Confirming Updated Data</h3>

<p>Verify your updated details by printing a fresh JAMB Selection Slip at the center. Log into your dashboard after 48 hours to confirm that the changes appear under your main candidate profile.</p>

<h3>Post-UTME Registration</h3>

<p>Once your updated choice reflects on the main system, visit your target school's screening portal. Follow local guidelines to submit your application details before their registration window closes.</p>

<h3>Uploading O'Level Results</h3>

<p>No tertiary institution in Nigeria can offer admission without verified secondary school results on the CAPS portal. Ensure your WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB grades are uploaded via an accredited CBT center.</p>
</section>
