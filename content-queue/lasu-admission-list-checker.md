<h2>1. Understanding the LASU Admission List Checker Architecture</h2>
<p>Lagos State University operates a multi-tiered admission processing system overseen by the University Senate and coordinated through the Central Admissions Clearance Committee. The admission portal does not publish static PDF documents containing thousands of names as was common in past decades. Instead, modern processing relies on database query tools where candidates input individual credentials to generate a personalized status report.</p>

<p>Official offers stem from strict adherence to regulatory standards established by the National Universities Commission and the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board. <p>When the university uploads provisional lists, data populates simultaneously on the local server managed by the LASU Information and Documentation Centre (LIDC) and the national database managed by JAMB. Candidate notifications roll out in structured batches rather than a single bulk release.</p>

<h3>Method A: Verification via the Official LASU LIDC Screening Portal</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Launch Your Web Browser:</strong> Open an updated version of Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Microsoft Edge.</li>
<li><strong>Navigate to the LIDC Portal:</strong> Enter the address <code>https://services.lidc.lasu.edu.ng/admissionscreening/</code> directly into your address bar. Alternatively, visit <code>https://www.lasu.edu.ng</code>, hover over the <strong>ADMISSIONS</strong> menu tab, and select <strong>UTME/DE Screening Status</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Input Primary Credentials:</strong> Locate the candidate login box on the landing interface. Enter your 10-digit <strong>JAMB Registration Number</strong> in the designated input field. Type your <strong>Surname</strong> in all capital letters matching your UTME registration profile.</li>
<li><strong>Execute Status Query:</strong> Click on the button labeled <strong>Submit</strong> or <strong>Check Screening Status</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Analyze Dashboard Display:</strong> The system retrieves your records from the database and displays your applicant summary. Read the message under the field titled <strong>Admission Status</strong>:
<ul>
<li><strong>Admitted:</strong> You have received a provisional offer of admission into the specified department.</li>
<li><strong>Recommended:</strong> Your file has cleared internal university screening and awaits final technical approval on JAMB CAPS.</li>
<li><strong>Screening Pending / In Progress:</strong> Your submitted O'Level results and UTME profile remain under evaluation by the faculty admissions officer.</li>
<li><strong>Not Admitted Yet:</strong> Your score falls below the current Merit batch threshold or your targeted department has not concluded processing.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>

<h3>Method B: Verification via JAMB Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS)</h3>
<p>State university offers remain invalid unless mirrored on the central JAMB database. To check your status on JAMB CAPS:</p>
<ol>
<li>Visit <code>https://efacility.jamb.gov.ng</code> on your computer or mobile device.</li>
<li>Log in using the email address and password registered during your UTME examination sign-up.</li>
<li>Switch your mobile browser view to <strong>Desktop Site Mode</strong> via browser settings to render the full side-navigation menu.</li>
<li>Click on <strong>Check Admission Status</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Access my CAPS</strong>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>Admission Status</strong> from the left sidebar navigation list.</li>
<li>Inspect the status response:
<ul>
<li><strong>CONGRATULATIONS:</strong> An offer exists. Click <strong>ACCEPT</strong> to lock in your offer. Do NOT click REJECT unless you intend to forfeit the seat permanently.</li>
<li><strong>ADMISSION IN PROGRESS (AIP):</strong> The university has submitted your name to JAMB; final approval is pending.</li>
<li><strong>RECOMMENDED:</strong> The institutional board has nominated your candidate file for final ratification.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</section>

<h2>4. Worked Mathematical Calculation of LASU Aggregate Scores</h2>
<p>Lagos State University uses an automated 50:50 composite scoring formula to rank candidates during screening. Your total screening aggregate equals 100 points, derived from combining your UTME score (50%) and your O'Level grades in five relevant subjects (50%).</p>

<p class="math-formula" style="text-align: center;">
TOTAL AGGREGATE SCORE (100%) = [UTME SCORE / 8] + [SUM OF TOP 5 O'LEVEL SUBJECT POINTS]
</p>

<p>O'Level point values apply to results obtained in WAEC (SSCE/GCE), NECO (SSCE/GCE), or NABTEB. Points are awarded based on performance in your single sitting or calculated across a maximum of two sittings according to university regulations:</p>

<p>O'Level Scoring Ceiling: 5 Subjects &times; 10 Points (A1) = 50 Points Max.<br>
UTME Scoring Ceiling: 400 UTME Max Score / 8 = 50 Points Max.<br>
Total Maximum Aggregate = 50 + 50 = 100 Points.</p>

<h2>5. Breakdown of Admission Lists: Merit, Catchment, ELDS &amp; Supplementary</h2>
<p>The admissions office processes candidates across distinct phases throughout the screening window. Checking your status requires understanding which phase current portal updates represent.</p>

<pre class="diagram-container">

<h3>1. The Merit Admission List (First Batch)</h3>
<p>The Merit list contains names of candidates who achieve high aggregate scores surpassing baseline departmental cut-off marks. Selection depends entirely on academic performance. State of origin plays no role in this initial phase.</p>

<h3>2. The Catchment Area List &amp; Lagos Indigene Verification Scheme (IIVS)</h3>
<p>As a Lagos State tertiary institution, statutory policy reserves 35% of entry spaces for verified Lagos State indigenes. However, claiming indigene status on your UTME form does not guarantee entry under this quota. Candidates must complete the Independent Indigene Verification Scheme (IIVS) clearance.</p>

<pre class="diagram-container">
+-----------------------------------+
|   IIVS CLEARANCE VERIFICATION     |
+-----------------------------------+
|
[Apply Online via lasu.edu.ng/iivs]
|
[Physical Verification Committee]
(Oba Alh. Hassan Sekoni Complex, Ojo)
|
+----------------+----------------+
|                                 |
v                                 v
[VERIFIED INDIGENE]               [UNVERIFIED / REJECTED]
|                                 |
Pushed to Catchment               Moved to General Non-Indigene
35% Quota Pool                    Competitive Pool
</pre>

<p>Unverified claims result in automatic disqualification from the catchment quota. The candidate's file then shifts back to the non-indigene general competitive pool.</p>

<h3>3. Educationally Less Developed States (ELDS) Quota</h3>
<p>In compliance with federal guidelines administered by JAMB, 20% of spaces accommodate candidates originating from states officially classified as educationally less developed. Candidates applying under ELDS must meet minimum statutory baseline requirements established by the Senate.</p>

<h3>4. Supplementary &amp; Change of Course Admission Lists</h3>
<p>Candidates who miss initial departmental Merit benchmarks can apply for supplementary consideration. When competitive courses like Law or Medicine exhaust their capacities, the university opens a Change of Course portal. This module allows qualified applicants to transfer their credentials to related departments where vacancies remain.</p>

<h3>5. Direct Entry (DE) Admission List</h3>
<p>Direct Entry applicants possessing diplomas from recognized institutions, A-Level IJMB/JUPEB qualifications, or NCE certificates undergo evaluation via a dedicated portal module. DE candidates enter directly into 200 Level upon verification of transcript records.</p>

<h3>Detailed Troubleshooting Steps</h3>

<h4>1. Issue: "No Admission Offered Yet" Displayed on Dashboard</h4>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Your composite aggregate score sits below the First Batch Merit cut-off mark, or your department has not uploaded candidate files to the database.<br>
<strong>Solution:</strong> Monitor both your LIDC dashboard and JAMB CAPS weekly. Departmental officers release supplementary batches progressively over six to eight weeks.</p>

<h4>2. Issue: Portal Displays "Invalid Password or Username"</h4>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Typing errors in your registration string or entering your surname in lowercase characters when uppercase formatting is required.<br>
<strong>Solution:</strong> Ensure your UTME Registration Number contains no spaces between digits and letters (e.g., <code>202610987654AB</code>). Enter your surname using all capital letters.</p>

<h4>3. Issue: Admitted on JAMB CAPS but Shown as "Not Admitted" on LASU Portal</h4>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Data synchronization lag between JAMB database servers and local university database tables.<br>
<strong>Solution:</strong> Accept the offer on JAMB CAPS first. Print your JAMB Provisional Admission Letter. Present these documents to the LASU Admissions Office during physical verification if the local portal lag exceeds seven business days.</p>

<h4>4. Issue: "O'Level Result Pending / Invalid Scratch Card" Notification</h4>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Automated verification scripts failed to read your WAEC/NECO result using the PIN and Serial Number provided during Post-UTME registration.<br>
<strong>Solution:</strong> Purchase a fresh WAEC/NECO result checking scratch card. Log into your screening profile and re-upload the card details accurately to trigger instant automated verification.</p>

<h4>5. Issue: IIVS Indigene Verification Rejection</h4>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Inconsistencies between local government identification letters and family historical claims presented to the verification panel.<br>
<strong>Solution:</strong> Report physically to the IIVS Secretariat at the Oba Hassan Sekoni Complex, Main Campus, Ojo, with original documents and chieftaincy endorsement letters for manual clearance.</p>

<h2>10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How do I use the LASU admission list checker?</summary>
<p>Visit the official screening portal at <code>services.lidc.lasu.edu.ng/admissionscreening/</code>. Enter your 10-digit JAMB Registration Number and your Surname in all capital letters into the user login fields, then click <strong>Submit</strong> to view your status.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Is the LASU 2026/2027 admission list officially out?</summary>
<p>The university releases admission offers in sequential batches (Merit, Catchment, Supplementary, Direct Entry) following approval by the Senate and JAMB. Track updates on both the official university website and the JAMB CAPS dashboard.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What is the difference between LASU and LASUED?</summary>
<p>Lagos State University (LASU) is a multi-disciplinary state institution established in 1983 with its main campus in Ojo. Lagos State University of Education (LASUED) is a specialized education university established in 2022, located in Oto/Ijanikin and Epe. They run separate application portals and selection processes.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What does "Admitted on JAMB CAPS but Not Admitted on LASU Portal" mean?</summary>
<p>This indicates that JAMB has processed and approved your provisional offer centrally, but the university's LIDC server has not completed its database sync. Your admission remains valid. Accept the offer on JAMB CAPS and keep your printout for clearance.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How does LASU calculate composite screening aggregate scores?</summary>
<p>LASU uses a 50:50 composite calculation model. Your UTME score is divided by 8 (yielding a maximum of 50 points), and your top five relevant O'Level subject grades are assigned point values (A1=10, B2=9, B3=8, C4=7, C5=6, C6=5) to yield another maximum of 50 points. Combining these yields your composite score out of 100%.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I gain admission into LASU without completing IIVS verification?</summary>
<p>If you are a Lagos State indigene, you must complete the Independent Indigene Verification Scheme (IIVS) clearance to be considered under the 35% Catchment Quota. If you skip or fail IIVS verification, your application defaults to the competitive Non-Indigene general pool.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What is LACACA clearance and is it mandatory?</summary>
<p>LACACA stands for the Lagos State University Central Admissions Clearance Application. It is a mandatory online document verification system. Admitted candidates must complete LACACA clearance to secure their matriculation numbers and retain their offers.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What should I do if my portal status displays "Screening Pending"?</summary>
<p>A "Screening Pending" message means your O'Level result PIN or submitted credentials are still under review by admissions officers. Verify that you entered valid examination scratch card details on your application portal.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How many sittings does LASU accept for O'Level results?</summary>
<p>LASU accepts a maximum of two sittings for O'Level results (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB) for most undergraduate programs. However, competitive programs like Medicine and Surgery (MBBS) may require a single-sitting result depending on annual senate guidelines.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How can I apply for a Change of Course if I miss my target aggregate?</summary>
<p>When the supplementary application window opens, log into your LIDC screening dashboard. Select the <strong>Change of Course</strong> module to view available departments matching your aggregate score, then submit your request online.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How much is the LASU acceptance fee for fresh undergraduate students?</summary>
<p>Acceptance fees are paid online through the official LASU Consult payment portal at <code>www.lasu.edu.ng/consult</code> after provisional admission is offered. Always verify current fee amounts on the official invoice generated with your JAMB registration number.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>What does "Admission in Progress (AIP)" mean on JAMB CAPS?</summary>
<p>"Admission in Progress" means the university has submitted your file to JAMB for review. Once JAMB officers complete their final technical checks, your status will update to "CONGRATULATIONS."</p>
</details>
</section>
