<h2>3. Open and Distance Learning (ODL) Mechanics at NOUN</h2>
<p>The National Open University of Nigeria operates as a single-mode open distance learning institution chartered by the Federal Government of Nigeria. Understanding how NOUN delivers lectures and conducts assessments prevents operational confusion during your academic journey.</p>

<h3>Open and Distance Learning Mechanics</h3>
<p>Open and Distance Learning removes traditional geographic and physical classroom constraints. Learning happens outside traditional lecture halls through digital portals, downloadable textbooks, self-instructional print modules, and electronic facilitation. NOUN does not maintain residential student hostels on campus.</p>

<h3>Digital Facilitation and Physical Study Centres</h3>
<p>NOUN combines digital instruction with physical support infrastructure:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Self-Instructional Courseware:</strong> Textbooks are written in interactive self-study modules available free of charge through the <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">NOUN eCourseware Download Guide</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Virtual Facilitation:</strong> Lecturers conduct live video streams and post recorded lectures via the university e-learning platform (<code>elearn.nouonline.net</code>).</li>
<li><strong>Physical Study Centres:</strong> Academic centres located in states across Nigeria serve as administrative touchpoints. Students visit study centres for document verification, ID card collection, laboratory practicals, and semester examinations. Check our <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">NOUN Study Centres Directory</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h3>The Flexible "Pay-As-You-Go" Course Structure</h3>
<p>Unlike conventional universities that demand full annual tuition upfront, NOUN utilizes a course-based payment system. Students pay a fixed compulsory semester fee, then register and pay per credit unit for individual courses based on personal budget.</p>

<pre>Total Semester Expense = Compulsory Semester Fee + Registered Course Credit Fees + Examination Registration Fees</pre>

<p>If financial constraints prevent registering for a full load in a given semester, an undergraduate can register for fewer credit units. Unregistered courses carry over to subsequent semesters without academic penalty, provided the student completes the degree within allowable university timeframes. Read our <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">NOUN Course Registration Guide</a>.</p>

<h3>Assessment Methods: TMAs and Final Exams</h3>
<p>Academic evaluation at NOUN relies on two primary assessment components:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Tutor-Marked Assignments (TMAs):</strong> Students complete three automated online assignments per course during the semester. TMAs account for 30% of the total score for that course. Read our complete <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">NOUN TMA Guide & Tips</a>.</li>
<li><strong>End-of-Semester Examinations:</strong> Final examinations constitute 70% of the course grade. Read our <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">NOUN Exam Preparation & POP Guide</a>.
<ul>
<li><strong>Electronic Exams (e-Exams):</strong> Computer-based assessments taken by 100-level and 200-level undergraduate students.</li>
<li><strong>Pen-on-Paper (POP) Exams:</strong> Written essay-style examinations taken at designated study centres by 300-level, 400-level, 500-level, and postgraduate students.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ol>
</section>

<section id="5-general-undergraduate-admission-requirements">
<h2>5. General Undergraduate Admission Requirements</h2>
<p>Eligibility rules vary according to the academic program and entry level. NOUN accepts credentials from recognized examination bodies, including WAEC, NECO, NABTEB, and GCE.</p>

<h3>Minimum Entry Criteria for All Programmes</h3>
<ol>
<li>Applicants must hold a minimum of five (5) credit passes in relevant subjects in WAEC, NECO, GCE, or NABTEB.</li>
<li>Credit passes must be obtained in no more than two (2) sittings.</li>
<li>Passes in <strong>English Language</strong> and <strong>Mathematics</strong> are mandatory for all Science, Social Science, Management Science, and Health Science courses.</li>
<li>Candidates applying for Arts or Humanities courses must possess a credit pass in English Language. A pass in Mathematics may be accepted for specific humanities fields, depending on departmental guidelines.</li>
</ol>

<h3>WAEC and NECO Result Combination Policy</h3>
<p>NOUN permits candidates to combine results from two separate sittings. For example, an applicant can combine WAEC 2023 and NECO 2024 to complete the required five O'Level credits. Read our detailed <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">WAEC Result Combination Rules</a> for verification procedures.</p>

<h3>Awaiting Results Policy</h3>
<p>Candidates applying with <strong>Awaiting Results (AR)</strong> can complete the online application pre-registration form. However, candidates must upload verified O'Level credit passes before physical screening clearance and matriculation number issuance at the study centre. Read our <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">Awaiting Result Verification Process</a> guide.</p>

<h3>Direct Entry Admission Requirements (200 Level / 300 Level)</h3>
<p>Candidates seeking advanced placement enter directly into 200 level or 300 level based on tertiary qualifications. Review our <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">Direct Entry Admission Criteria</a>:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>National Certificate in Education (NCE):</strong> Merit pass in two major subject areas relevant to the chosen Education degree grants 200-level entry.</li>
<li><strong>National Diploma (ND):</strong> Minimum of Upper Credit or Lower Credit from a recognized polytechnic in a related field grants 200-level entry.</li>
<li><strong>Higher National Diploma (HND):</strong> Pass or Lower Credit grants 200-level entry. Upper Credit or Distinction in a closely aligned program grants 300-level entry into specific management or computing fields.</li>
<li><strong>First Degree (B.A./B.Sc.):</strong> A recognized bachelor's degree in a non-related discipline admits candidates into the 200 level of a new degree program.</li>
</ul>

<h2>6. Requirements for Professional Programmes</h2>
<p>Certain professional faculties mandate specialized entrance criteria set by professional registration boards alongside National Universities Commission (NUC) baselines.</p>

<h3>Bachelor of Nursing Science (B.NSc.)</h3>
<p>The Bachelor of Nursing Science program at NOUN is designed as an academic development pathway for practicing nurses. The program operates under accreditation monitoring by the <strong>National Universities Commission (NUC)</strong> and the <strong>Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN)</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Academic Qualifications:</strong> Five O'Level credit passes in WAEC, NECO, or GCE at no more than two sittings. Compulsory subjects:
<ul>
<li>English Language</li>
<li>Mathematics</li>
<li>Physics</li>
<li>Chemistry</li>
<li>Biology</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Professional Registration:</strong> Applicants must be registered with the Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria as a <strong>Registered Nurse (RN)</strong> or <strong>Registered Midwife (RM)</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Practicing License:</strong> A valid, current practicing license or PIN issued by the NMCN is required.</li>
<li><strong>Clinical Attestation:</strong> Candidates must submit an official letter of attestation from their current healthcare employer or clinical supervisor.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Bachelor of Laws (LL.B)</h3>
<p>The Faculty of Law offers the Bachelor of Laws (LL.B) degree program.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>O'Level Requirements:</strong> Five credit passes in WAEC, NECO, or GCE at no more than two sittings, including:
<ul>
<li>English Language</li>
<li>Literature in English</li>
<li>Mathematics</li>
<li>Two Social Science or Arts subjects (e.g., Government, Economics, Commerce, CRK/IRS)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Direct Entry Law Requirements:</strong> A first degree from an accredited university with at least Second Class Lower division, or a Diploma in Law with Merit standing from a recognized institution.</li>
<li><strong>Law School Status Notice:</strong> Following the National Open University of Nigeria (Amendment) Act 2018 and regulatory decisions by the Council of Legal Education (CLE), special Bar Part I and Bar Part II remedial cohorts were organized for NOUN law graduates. Subsequent admissions into the Nigerian Law School remain subject to ongoing CLE quota allocations, Bar Part II advert schedules, and official notices issued by the Council of Legal Education.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Public Health and Environmental Health Science</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>B.Sc. Public Health:</strong> Requires five O'Level credits in English, Mathematics, Chemistry, Biology, and Physics. Registered Community Health Extension Workers (CHEW) with Higher Diplomas qualify for Direct Entry into 200 level.</li>
<li><strong>B.Sc. Environmental Health Science:</strong> Requires five credits in English, Mathematics, Chemistry, Physics, and Biology/Health Science. Environmental Health Technician diplomas are accepted for Direct Entry.</li>
</ul>
</section>

<h3>1. Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) Programmes</h3>

<li>Lower Credit or Merit pass in Higher National Diploma (HND) from a recognized polytechnic.</li>

</ul>

<h3>2. Master's Degree Programmes (M.A., M.Sc., M.Ed., MBA)</h3>
<ul>
<li>A First Degree in a relevant field with at least Second Class Lower division from an accredited university.</li>
<li>Candidates with a Third Class degree or HND must possess an accredited Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) in a related discipline with a minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 3.0 on a 5.0 scale.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3. Doctor of Philosophy (Ph.D.) Programmes</h3>
<ul>
<li>A Master's degree in a relevant discipline from an accredited university with a minimum CGPA of 3.50 on a 5.0 scale or a weighted average score of 60%.</li>
<li>Submission and successful defense of a research proposal during departmental screening.</li>
</ul>

<ol>
<li>Confirm that your uploaded O'Level subjects meet the minimum entry requirements for the target faculty.</li>
<li>Log into your student dashboard and select <strong>Services</strong> > <strong>Apply for Change of Programme</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose the new target <strong>Faculty</strong> and <strong>Degree Programme</strong>.</li>
<li>Pay the ₦5,000 administrative processing fee via your portal e-Wallet.</li>
<li>Upon departmental approval, the system updates your academic catalog. Previously completed general studies (GST) courses with passing grades carry over automatically to your new transcript.</li>
</ol>

<h3>3. Deferring Your Semester Studies</h3>
<p>Because NOUN operates a flexible ODL system, candidates do not need to submit formal deferment letters to take a hiatus. If financial or personal commitments arise:</p>
<ul>
<li>Simply refrain from paying the compulsory semester fee and course registration fees for that active term.</li>
<li>Your student portal account remains active in a paused state.</li>
<li>When ready to resume, log in during any future open semester, load your e-Wallet, pay the active compulsory fee, and resume course registration without academic penalty or re-application fees.</li>
</ul>

<h2>19. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</h2>

<h3>Does NOUN require JAMB UTME for admission?</h3>
<p>No. The National Open University of Nigeria does not require candidates to sit for or pass the JAMB UTME examination. Admission relies on O'Level credit passes or higher qualification diplomas.</p>

<h3>Are NOUN degrees recognized by the NUC?</h3>
<p>Yes. The National Open University of Nigeria is a fully accredited federal institution established by an Act of the National Assembly and regulated by the National Universities Commission (NUC).</p>

<h3>Can I study at NOUN while working full-time?</h3>
<p>Yes. NOUN's Open and Distance Learning model is specifically designed for working professionals, offering online e-courseware, flexible virtual classes, and weekend study centre support.</p>

<h3>Does NOUN have student hostels on campus?</h3>
<p>No. NOUN operates an open distance learning model without residential student hostels. Students live off-campus and visit study centres primarily for screening, practicals, and exams.</p>

<h3>Can I transfer from NOUN to another university or vice versa?</h3>
<p>Yes. Students can transfer to NOUN by applying for Direct Entry and submitting official academic transcripts. Transferring from NOUN to conventional universities depends on the receiving institution's transfer policy.</p>

<h3>Can I combine WAEC and NECO results for NOUN admission?</h3>
<p>Yes. NOUN permits candidates to combine credit passes from two separate sittings across WAEC, NECO, GCE, or NABTEB examinations.</p>

<h3>Does NOUN accept NABTEB O'Level results?</h3>
<p>Yes. NOUN accepts NABTEB National Technical Certificate (NTC) or National Business Certificate (NBC) results with five credit passes in relevant subjects.</p>

<h3>How long does the NOUN admission process take?</h3>
<p>Under standard conditions, online application pre-registration takes approximately 30 minutes once payment is confirmed. Physical document verification at the study centre typically spans 1 to 3 business days, depending on applicant volume.</p>

<h3>Does NOUN accept awaiting results during application?</h3>
<p>You can select awaiting results while filling out the pre-registration form. However, final physical clearance and matriculation number generation require uploading verified O'Level credit results.</p>

<h3>Can NOUN nursing graduates register with the Nursing Council?</h3>
<p>Yes. The Bachelor of Nursing Science (B.NSc.) degree is accredited by the NUC and Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN), enabling graduates to upgrade their professional status.</p>

<h3>Who needs to pay the JAMB Regularization fee at NOUN?</h3>
<p>JAMB Regularization applies to admitted undergraduate students who enrolled without a JAMB registration number. This process regularizes their admission records with JAMB for official mobilization or exemption through the NYSC.</p>

<h3>Can foreign or international students apply to NOUN?</h3>
<p>Yes. Foreign candidates living in Nigeria or within regions covered by international study centres can apply online by submitting evaluated academic credentials.</p>

<h3>Can I change my study centre or degree programme after admission?</h3>
<p>Yes. Students can apply for a change of study centre or change of programme by submitting a formal request through their student portal dashboard, subject to departmental approval.</p>

<h3>Is lecture attendance compulsory at NOUN?</h3>
<p>Physical lecture attendance is not compulsory for standard theoretical courses due to virtual facilitation. However, attendance is mandatory for laboratory practicals, clinical postings, teaching practice, and semester examinations.</p>

<h3>How many TMAs are required per course, and what happens if I miss one?</h3>
<p>Each course requires three Tutor-Marked Assignments (TMA 1, TMA 2, TMA 3). Missing a TMA reduces your continuous assessment score, as TMAs collectively constitute 30% of your final course grade.</p>

<h3>Can I fast-track my degree and graduate earlier than 4 years?</h3>
<p>While NOUN allows self-paced registration, students must fulfill total minimum credit unit graduation requirements prescribed by the NUC. Undergraduates cannot exceed the 24-credit unit ceiling per semester, meaning a 4-year degree requires a minimum of 8 active academic semesters.</p>

<h3>How do I request official transcripts from NOUN?</h3>
<p>Graduates can request official academic transcripts online through the NOUN Transcript Portal (<code>transcripts.nou.edu.ng</code>). Transcripts are processed and sent directly to requesting foreign evaluation agencies (such as WES) or postgraduate institutions.</p>

<h3>When does NOUN hold its Convocation Ceremony?</h3>
<p>NOUN holds an annual national convocation ceremony, typically in March or April, at the University Headquarters in Jabi, Abuja, with decentralized viewing streams across major zonal study centres.</p>
