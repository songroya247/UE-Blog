<section id="section-2">
<h2>2. Understanding UNIBEN Admission Philosophy & CBT Mechanics</h2>
<p>The University of Benin uses a standardized Computer-Based Test to evaluate candidate readiness. Because grading standards vary across secondary schools, UNIBEN relies on its Central Entrance Examination Committee (CEEC) to set a single evaluation standard at its Ugbowo campus.</p>

<blockquote>
<strong>Historical Assessment Note:</strong><br>
Historically, Post UTME test questions have generally carried equal marks across all four subjects, unless explicitly communicated otherwise by the university during a specific session.
</blockquote>

<h3>UTME vs. UNIBEN CBT Screening</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>JAMB UTME:</strong> 180 questions taken over 120 minutes across nationwide test hubs.</li>
<li><strong>UNIBEN Post UTME:</strong> 60 questions taken over 60 minutes at the Ugbowo Main Campus in Benin City.</li>
</ul>
</section>

<h3>UTME Benchmark & Choice Constraints</h3>

Candidates who listed UNIBEN as a second choice must complete a <a href="/posts/jamb-change-of-institution-portal">JAMB Change of Institution</a> to make UNIBEN their primary choice before portal registration.</p>

<section id="section-4">
<h2>4. Step-by-Step Application Guide</h2>

<h3>Step 1: Accessing the Kofa Portal</h3>
<p>Go directly to <code>unibenportal.com</code>. Avoid entering password details on third-party referral blogs.</p>

<div class="image-placeholder">
[IMAGE RECOMMENDATION: Screenshot of the UNIBEN Kofa Portal home screen highlighting the Student Portal dropdown]<br>
<em>Alt text:</em> "Homepage of the UNIBEN Kofa Portal showing the menu links for student applications."
</div>

<h3>Step 2: Creating Your Account</h3>
<ol>
<li>Select <strong>Student Portal</strong>, click <strong>Applications</strong>, and choose <strong>Post UTME 2026/2027</strong>.</li>
<li>Type your official JAMB registration number to pull your records from the central database.</li>
<li>Provide an active email address and phone number.</li>
<li>Check your inbox for your system-generated User ID and Password.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Step 3: Bio-Data & Academic Entry</h3>
<p>Log into the portal using your credentials. Carefully enter your personal details and O'Level grades.</p>

<blockquote>
<strong>Common Registration Mistakes:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Entering an email address you cannot access.</li>
<li>Arranging names differently from your official JAMB profile.</li>
<li>Entering incorrect O'Level grades (e.g., entering A1 instead of C6).</li>
<li>Uploading blurry document scans.</li>
</ul>
</blockquote>

<h3>Step 4: Remita Payment & Troubleshooting</h3>
<p>The standard screening fee is N2,000, as regulated by federal guidelines. Additional portal administrative charges usually bring the total to around N3,000–N3,500.</p>

<ol>
<li>Click <strong>Add Payment Slip</strong> and select <strong>Pay with Remita</strong>.</li>
<li>Note your 12-digit Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR) number.</li>
<li>Pay online using your debit card, or take the RRR slip to any commercial bank branch.</li>
</ol>

<h4>Handling Payment Timeouts</h4>
<p>If your bank account is debited but the application page shows "Unpaid", do not make a second payment right away. Log out, wait 30 to 60 minutes for inter-bank clearance, return to your payment page, and click <strong>Re-Query Payment</strong>.</p>

<div class="image-placeholder">
[IMAGE RECOMMENDATION: Screenshot of the Remita RRR invoice page displaying payment details and transaction reference]<br>
<em>Alt text:</em> "Sample Remita payment page generated on the UNIBEN portal showing the RRR code."
</div>

<h3>Step 5: Passport Photograph Upload</h3>
<p>Based on previous session guidelines, passport uploads usually require:</p>
<ul>
<li>Plain red background.</li>
<li>Clear face view without tinted glasses or heavy headgear.</li>
<li>Maximum image file size under 100 KB in JPEG format.</li>
</ul>
<p><em>Note: Always review the current portal screen for any updated photo parameters.</em></p>

<h3>Step 6: Confirmation & Slip Printing</h3>
<p>Check every detail carefully. Once satisfied, click <strong>Final Submission</strong>. Print two copies of your registration acknowledgment slip for physical verification on test day.</p>

<h3>Hall Procedures & Technical Issues</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Security Screening:</strong> Security officials check your printed registration slip against your ID at the gate.</li>
<li><strong>Terminal Login:</strong> Enter your registration credentials at your assigned computer station.</li>
<li><strong>Timer:</strong> The on-screen clock counts down from 60 minutes once you start.</li>
<li><strong>Technical Failures:</strong> If your computer freezes or turns off during the test, notify an invigilator immediately by raising your hand. Your progress is stored on the central server, and your remaining time resumes once you are logged into a backup machine.</li>
</ol>

<section id="section-7">
<h2>7. Aggregate Score Calculation</h2>
<p>In recent admission cycles, UNIBEN has commonly weighted UTME and Post UTME scores equally when calculating the overall aggregate score out of 100%. Candidates should confirm the current session's exact calculation methodology if an official formula notice is released.</p>

<blockquote>
<h3>HISTORICAL AGGREGATE FORMULA EXAMPLE</h3>
<p><code>Aggregate Score = (JAMB Score / 8) + (Post-UTME Score / 2)</code></p>
<p><strong>Sample Calculation:</strong><br>
JAMB Score = 264 &rarr; 264 / 8 = 33.00<br>
PUTME Score = 70 &rarr; 70 / 2 = 35.00<br>
<strong>Calculated Aggregate:</strong> 33.00 + 35.00 = 68.00%</p>
</blockquote>
</section>

<section id="section-10">
<h2>10. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)</h2>

<h3>Can I combine WAEC and NECO results for UNIBEN?</h3>
<p>Yes, UNIBEN generally permits combining WAEC and NECO results for most disciplines. However, the College of Medical Sciences strictly requires five credit passes obtained in a single sitting.</p>

<h3>Does UNIBEN accept NABTEB results?</h3>
<p>NABTEB results are accepted for engineering, natural sciences, and management courses. They are generally not accepted for Law or Medical Sciences.</p>

<h3>Can I apply with awaiting O'Level results?</h3>
<p>Yes, you can apply using the awaiting result option. However, your final grades must be uploaded to JAMB CAPS and the Kofa portal before admission processing concludes.</p>

<h3>Can I edit my registration details after submitting?</h3>
<p>No. Once your application is finally submitted, fields become locked. Critical corrections must be requested in person at the UNIBEN ICT Center before registration closes.</p>

<h3>Can I change my course after submitting my application?</h3>
<p>You cannot change your course on the UNIBEN portal after submission. If your final score misses your target department, UNIBEN may offer an alternative program through the JAMB CAPS transfer framework.</p>

<h3>Does UNIBEN accept second-choice candidates?</h3>
<p>No. UNIBEN processes applications exclusively for candidates who selected the institution as their <strong>First Choice</strong> on JAMB.</p>

<h3>What happens if I miss my CBT screening schedule?</h3>
<p>The university rarely reschedules missed tests. Candidates who miss their assigned batch time typically forfeit the screening portion of their application score.</p>

<h3>Do Direct Entry candidates take the CBT screening test?</h3>
<p>Direct Entry screening policies vary by session. Historically, UNIBEN conducts an aptitude test or candidate verification exercise for DE applicants. Always check your printed registration slip for specific session directives.</p>
</section>
