<section id="understanding-screening">
<h2>1. Understanding the UNN Post-UTME Screening Framework</h2>

<h3>What is Post-UTME and Why is it Conducted?</h3>
<p>The Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board conducts standardized entrance testing across Nigeria. Federal institutions receive thousands of applications exceeding their physical capacity. The University of Nigeria uses the Post-UTME screening exercise as an independent assessment system. Screening verifies student identities and evaluates academic competence. High test scores prove that candidates possess the analytical skills required for demanding degree programs.</p>

<pre>
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                   UNN ADMISSION SELECTION FUNNEL                  |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. UTME Candidate Selection (Must choose UNN as 1st Choice)       |
|    └─ Minimum Baseline Cut-off Score: 160                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 2. Post-UTME Portal Registration & Document Verification          |
|    └─ O'Level Upload to JAMB CAPS & UNN Portal (Max 100KB files) |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 3. Computer-Based Screening Examination (CBT)                     |
|    └─ 60 Questions | 60 Minutes | 4 Core UTME Subjects          |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 4. Aggregate Score Calculation & Merit List Publication           |
|    └─ Primary List -&gt; Supplementary List -&gt; Advert List           |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------+
</pre>

<h3>UNN's Admission Philosophy and Screening Objectives</h3>
<p>Established in 1955 and officially opened in 1960, the institution operates under the motto "To Restore the Dignity of Man." University admissions policy prioritizes academic merit, national diversity, and consistent secondary performance. Screening filters out credential fraud while selecting applicants who show intellectual potential. The university reviews secondary school grades alongside entrance test scores to construct a comprehensive candidate assessment.</p>

<h3>Key Differences: UTME vs. Direct Entry (DE) Screening</h3>
<p>Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination applicants complete a 60-minute computer-based exam. Direct Entry candidates enter directly into 200 Level degree programs based on advanced qualifications. Direct Entry applicants do not write the written entrance exam during most admissions cycles. Direct Entry candidates submit Advanced Level certificates, Higher National Diplomas, Nigeria Certificates in Education, or JUPEB transcripts. The university registry checks these credentials against issuing institutions to establish merit placement lists.</p>

<h3>O'Level Requirements and Sitting Limitations</h3>
<p>Candidates must possess five credit passes in relevant secondary school subjects. English Language and Mathematics are mandatory across all faculties. Acceptable examination bodies include WAEC, NECO, and NABTEB. Applicants submitting a single O'Level sitting earn a weighted point advantage during aggregate selection. Combining two sittings is permitted, but both result statements must satisfy departmental subject rules. Candidates with awaiting secondary results cannot complete portal registration.</p>

<h3>Direct Entry (DE) Eligibility</h3>
<p>Direct Entry applicants must present recognized post-secondary qualifications. Qualifying diplomas and certificates include:</p>
<ul>
<li>National Diploma (ND) with Upper Credit minimum standing.</li>
<li>Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE) with credit passes in two main teaching subjects.</li>
<li>JUPEB or IJMB certificates with specific grade point thresholds.</li>
<li>GCE Advanced Level passes in two or three relevant subjects.</li>
</ul>
<p>Direct Entry candidates must instruct their former institutions to send official academic transcripts directly to the Registrar's department. Failure to submit verified transcripts results in application disqualification.</p>

<h3>Special Considerations, Age Thresholds, and Name Alignment</h3>
<p>University admissions regulations strictly enforce age requirements. Applicants must attain 16 years of age by September 30 of the entry year. Candidates under 16 face automatic system rejection during registration. Name consistency across your JAMB registration profile, O'Level certificates, and birth documents is compulsory. Name order discrepancies prevent identity verification.</p>

<h2>3. UNN Aggregate Score Calculation Formulas &amp; Worked Examples</h2>

<p>The university uses explicit mathematical formulas to determine candidate merit rankings. Calculation methodologies adapt depending on whether physical computer-based testing is administered or an online document evaluation model is applied.</p>

<h3>Method A: The CBT-Based 50:50 Weighting Model</h3>
<p>When computer-based examinations take place, the overall aggregate score combines your JAMB result and Post-UTME score in an equal 50:50 ratio.</p>

<pre>
STANDARD CBT AGGREGATE FORMULA:
Aggregate Score = (UTME Score / 8) + (Post-UTME Score / 2)

Where:
- UTME Score is over 400 (Maximum Contribution = 50 Points)
- Post-UTME Score is over 100 (Maximum Contribution = 50 Points)
</pre>

<h3>Method B: The Online Screening (90:10 O'Level + JAMB) Point Model</h3>
<p>When physical examinations are suspended, the university uses a weighted point system. JAMB performance accounts for 90% of the total score, while verified O'Level grades contribute 10%.</p>

<pre>
ALTERNATIVE SCREENING FORMULA (90:10 MODEL):
Aggregate Score = [(UTME Score * 0.9) / 4] + Total O'Level Subject Points
</pre>

<section id="step-by-step-portal">
<h2>4. Step-by-Step Portal Registration Guide (unnportal.unn.edu.ng)</h2>

<p>Completing your online application demands total accuracy. Follow this step-by-step procedure to ensure proper registration on the university portal.</p>

<pre>
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
|               UNN PORTAL REGISTRATION WORKFLOW                     |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Visit official portal at unnportal.unn.edu.ng                   |
| 2. Select "Prospective Students" -&gt; "Post-UTME Application"        |
| 3. Input 10-digit JAMB Registration Number to fetch candidate data |
| 4. Generate Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR) Invoice               |
| 5. Pay N2,000 application fee + N3,300 service &amp; processing fee    |
| 6. Re-login to input O'Level grades &amp; upload scanned 100KB files   |
| 7. Verify entry details and print Acknowledgement Slip             |
+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
</pre>

<h3>Step 1: Checking Your Eligibility and Admission Status</h3>
<p>Open your internet browser and visit <code>unn.edu.ng</code> or <code>unnportal.unn.edu.ng</code>. Confirm that your UTME score satisfies the institutional baseline threshold of 160. Verify that the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board portal records the University of Nigeria as your first-choice institution. Process a change of institution on the JAMB portal prior to registration if you originally chose another university.</p>

<h3>Step 2: Accessing the UNN Post UTME Portal</h3>
<p>Click on the <strong>Prospective Students</strong> tab located on the main portal menu. Select <strong>Online Application Form</strong> for the current academic session. Input your valid JAMB registration number into the text field. The system retrieves your basic candidate records directly from central matriculation databases.</p>

<h3>Step 3: Generating and Paying for the Screening Fee via Remita</h3>
<p>The system generates an official payment invoice containing a unique Remita Retrieval Reference (RRR). Fee details break down as follows:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Application Form Fee:</strong> ₦2,000 (Standard national fee)</li>
<li><strong>Portal Processing &amp; Service Charge:</strong> ₦3,300</li>
<li><strong>Total Required Fee:</strong> ₦5,300</li>
</ul>

<p>Select your preferred payment channel:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Online Card Payment:</strong> Pay immediately using Interswitch, Mastercard, or Visa debit cards on the Remita online gateway.</li>
<li><strong>Commercial Bank Branch:</strong> Print the invoice slip containing your RRR code and present it to tellers at any major bank nationwide. Collect a stamped receipt upon payment.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Step 4: Completing the Online Application Form</h3>
<p>Log back into <code>unnportal.unn.edu.ng</code> after payment validation. Enter your JAMB registration number and RRR receipt code to unlock application fields. Complete all required sections:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Personal Data:</strong> Verify date of birth, state of origin, local government area, and contact details.</li>
<li><strong>Academic Credentials:</strong> Enter exact subject grades for your WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB sittings.</li>
<li><strong>Passport Photo Upload:</strong> Upload a recent digital passport photograph with a clear white background.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Step 5: Submitting Your Application and Printing Confirmation</h3>
<p>Review every entry carefully before final submission. Typos in grade entries trigger application disqualification. Submit the form to generate your official UNN Post-UTME Registration Slip. Print two color copies on clean A4 paper. Save a digital PDF copy on your personal drive for future verification checks.</p>

<p>Backup industrial generators power all university computer testing centers. If your assigned computer terminal freezes or shuts down, remain calm. Raise your hand quietly to notify a hall supervisor. System servers record answer progress automatically. Your examination timer pauses immediately and resumes when your session is restored on a replacement terminal.</p>
</section>

<h2>9. Post-Screening Operations &amp; Admission List Tracking</h2>

<p>The admissions cycle transitions into score computation and list compilation after tests conclude.</p>

<h3>How and When Results Are Released</h3>
<p>Screening scores are published on the portal shortly after test completion. Log into <code>unnportal.unn.edu.ng</code> using your JAMB registration number and payment reference code to view your verified screening result.</p>

<h3>Understanding Official Admission Batches</h3>
<ol>
<li><strong>Primary Merit Admission List:</strong> Features top-ranking candidates who meet early departmental merit scores.</li>
<li><strong>Supplementary Admission List:</strong> Accommodates eligible candidates who missed initial merit cut-offs but satisfy departmental quotas.</li>
<li><strong>Advert / Shopping List:</strong> Enables applicants to transfer from over-subscribed faculties into alternative courses with available openings.</li>
</ol>

<h3>JAMB CAPS Monitoring and Acceptance Procedures</h3>
<p>Check your profile status on the JAMB Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) regularly. When offered admission, click **ACCEPT** within two weeks to secure your place. Clicking reject forfeits the admission offer. Print your official JAMB Admission Letter immediately after accepting the offer.</p>

<section id="faqs">
<h2>11. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)</h2>

<h3>What happens if I miss my scheduled screening session?</h3>
<p>The university strictly enforces examination schedules. Candidates who miss assigned sessions forfeit their screening attempt unless university authorities organize an official mop-up examination. Submit a formal letter to the Registrar's office immediately if severe illness or documented emergencies prevent attendance.</p>

<h3>How do I correct errors in my application form after submission?</h3>
<p>Data corrections are permissible on the portal before registration closes. Access the correction portal at <code>unnportal.unn.edu.ng</code> and authenticate your record using your payment receipt code. O'Level grade entries cannot be edited after portal closure.</p>

<h3>Can I change my course after registering for Post-UTME?</h3>
<p>Course transfers are allowed when the university opens its advert application window. Candidates whose aggregate scores fall below primary departmental targets can purchase an advert form on the portal to switch into available alternative courses.</p>

<h3>How do social media groups assist prospective applicants during registration?</h3>
<p>Student candidate communities share real-time updates regarding screening dates, venue changes, and portal status. Always cross-check information shared across candidate groups against official notices on <code>unn.edu.ng</code> to prevent fraud.</p>

<h3>What is the difference between UNN general cut-off and departmental cut-off?</h3>
<p>The general baseline cut-off (160) is the minimum score required to register on the portal. The departmental cut-off is the final aggregate score required to gain admission into a specific degree program. Departmental scores vary annually based on applicant performance and quotas.</p>

<h3>What happens if my O'Level results are withheld during registration?</h3>
<p>Candidates must possess verified O'Level credit passes before portal registration closes. If your results are withheld by WAEC or NECO, you cannot complete registration. Upload confirmed grades to JAMB CAPS as soon as results are released.</p>
</section>
