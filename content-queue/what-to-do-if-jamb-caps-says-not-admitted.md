<p>You logged into your Central Admissions Processing System portal, opened the admission status tab, and saw three words displayed in bold text: "NOT ADMITTED." Your heart dropped instantly. Thousands of Nigerian tertiary institution applicants face this exact screen every single admission cycle. You are likely asking yourself what to do if JAMB CAPS says not admitted when your UTME score seemed high enough for your preferred course.</p>

<p>Seeing this notification does not mean your academic journey for the current session has ended. The Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) operates as a dynamic, multi-tier clearinghouse. Processing admission recommendations takes months, moving through institutional screening boards, departmental admission committees, and Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) desk officers. Understanding why your dashboard displays this message—and taking immediate, strategic steps—can reverse your status before the matriculation window closes.</p>

<h3>1.1 What Does "Not Admitted" Literally Mean?</h3>
<p>The phrase "Not Admitted" is the default system state on the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board portal. Every candidate starts with this status the moment their profile is created for an academic cycle. It simply indicates that no tertiary institution has cleared your candidate profile for an official offer, and JAMB has not approved a matriculation spot for your registration number.</p>

<p>This text remains unchanged while your institution reviews screening files. It does not mean you failed your screening examination. It does not mean your chosen institution rejected your application permanently. It means the system has not logged a formal recommendation code from the university registrar to the JAMB matriculation database.</p>

<h3>1.2 Why You See "Not Admitted" Instead of Other Statuses</h3>
<p>The admissions portal updates through explicit manual uploads executed by tertiary institution desk officers. A university must screen candidate profiles in batches before pushing updates to the national database.</p>

<h3>1.3 The Difference Between "Not Admitted" and "Admission in Progress"</h3>
<p>"Not Admitted" means your file sits in the general candidate pool. The institution's admission committee has either not evaluated your record or has not transmitted your serial number to the central system.</p>

<p>"Admission in Progress" (AIP) indicates that your institution cleared your credentials. The university admission officer submitted your name to JAMB for final audit. When your status reads AIP, your spot is essentially reserved, provided your uploaded O'Level credentials match national regulatory quotas (such as NUC, MDCN, CLE, or NMCN guidelines).</p>

<h3>1.4 Common Misconceptions About the "Not Admitted" Status</h3>
<p>Candidates often fall victim to inaccurate campus rumors regarding how the portal updates. Clarifying these fallacies prevents unnecessary panic during screening months:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Myth 1:</strong> <em>"Not Admitted means the university finished admitting students for the year."</em><br>
Fact: Universities release admission offers in staggered batches (Merit List, Catchment List, ELDS List, Supplementary List, Direct Entry List). "Not Admitted" remains visible while subsequent lists undergo processing.</li>
<li><strong>Myth 2:</strong> <em>"If my status says Not Admitted, I must write the next UTME."</em><br>
Fact: Admission cycles run across several months. Candidates often transition from "Not Admitted" to "Congratulations" during late-stage supplementary processing.</li>
<li><strong>Myth 3:</strong> <em>"JAMB decides who gets admitted without university input."</em><br>
Fact: The institution conducts the primary screening and submits recommended candidates to JAMB. JAMB verifies that candidates meet national quotas before issuing final approval.</li>
</ul>

<h2>2. Common Reasons Your JAMB CAPS Status is "Not Admitted"</h2>

<h3>2.1 Admission Slot Allocation & Institutional Processes</h3>
<p>Federal and state universities operate under strict capacity caps imposed by regulatory authorities. The National Universities Commission (NUC), Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN), Council of Legal Education (CLE), and Nursing and Midwifery Council of Nigeria (NMCN) assign fixed admission quotas to every department.</p>

<p>If a university Department of Nursing Science holds an NMCN approval limit of 100 students, the institution cannot admit 101 candidates. Even if 1,500 candidates score above 250 in the UTME, only the top 100 aggregate performers earn recommendation slots. The remaining 1,400 applicants retain the "Not Admitted" status regardless of their strong scores.</p>

<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>

+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  [ MERIT CATEGORY: 45% ]  -> Open to top performers nationwide                |
|  [ CATCHMENT AREA: 35% ]  -> Reserved for candidate's state of origin zone     |
|  [ ELDS CATEGORY:  20% ]  -> Reserved for Educationally Less Developed States |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
</div>

<h3>2.2 Your Academic Profile: UTME, Post-UTME, and O'Levels</h3>

<h4>2.2.1 Score Thresholds & Cut-Off Marks</h4>
<p>Meeting the institutional baseline cut-off mark does not guarantee admission into your chosen program. Universities establish separate, highly competitive departmental aggregate scores based on applicant volume.</p>

<p>Calculations vary by institution. Here is how top Nigerian universities calculate aggregate admission scores:</p>

<h5>Formula A: 50% UTME + 30% Post-UTME + 20% O'Level (e.g., UNILAG Model)</h5>
<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
Aggregate Score = (UTME Score / 8) + (Post-UTME Score / 1) + O'Level Points
</div>

<p><strong>O'Level Grading Scale (Top 5 relevant subjects):</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>A1 = 4.0 points</li>
<li>B2 = 3.6 points</li>
<li>B3 = 3.2 points</li>
<li>C4 = 2.8 points</li>
<li>C5 = 2.4 points</li>
<li>C6 = 2.0 points</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Worked Calculation Example:</strong><br>
A candidate applies for Computer Science at UNILAG.<br>
• UTME Score = 280<br>
• Post-UTME Score = 22 / 30<br>
• O'Level Grades: Mathematics (A1), English (B2), Physics (B3), Chemistry (B2), Further Mathematics (C4).</p>

<p>UTME Component = 280 / 8 = 35.0<br>
Post-UTME Component = 22.0<br>
O'Level Points = 4.0 + 3.6 + 3.2 + 3.6 + 2.8 = 17.2<br>
<strong>Total Aggregate = 35.0 + 22.0 + 17.2 = 74.2%</strong></p>

<p>If the departmental aggregate threshold for Computer Science is set at 76.5% (<em>Historical Admission Trend</em>), this candidate's status remains "Not Admitted" despite their strong 280 UTME score.</p>

<h5>Formula B: 50% UTME + 50% Post-UTME (e.g., UI Model)</h5>
<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
Aggregate Score = (UTME Score / 8) + (Post-UTME Score / 2)
</div>

<p><strong>Worked Calculation Example:</strong><br>
A candidate applies for Law at the University of Ibadan.<br>
• UTME Score = 290<br>
• Post-UTME Score = 68 / 100</p>

<p>UTME Component = 290 / 8 = 36.25<br>
Post-UTME Component = 68 / 2 = 34.00<br>
<strong>Total Aggregate = 36.25 + 34.00 = 70.25%</strong></p>

<p>If the departmental merit threshold for Law is 72.8% (<em>Historical Admission Trend</em>), the status stays "Not Admitted."</p>

<h3>2.3 Course Competition & Program Popularity</h3>
<p>Programs like Medicine, Pharmacy, Nursing, Law, Computer Science, and Mechanical Engineering receive an overwhelming volume of applications relative to available slots.</p>

<p>Consider an institution receiving 12,000 first-choice applications for Medicine and Surgery, where NUC and MDCN set the admission quota at 150. Even candidates scoring 290 in UTME may miss out on the top 150 aggregate spots. Their status will show "Not Admitted" unless they apply for a change of course to an allied medical field like Physiology, Anatomy, or Public Health.</p>

<h3>2.4 JAMB's Systemic Processes: Merit, Catchment, ELDS</h3>
<p>Federal universities operate under a mandatory policy framework to ensure equitable national representation:</p>

<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                          FEDERAL ADMISSION ALLOCATION RULE                             |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. MERIT (45%): Open to top aggregate candidates regardless of state of origin.        |
|                                                                                        |
| 2. CATCHMENT (35%): Reserved for candidates originating from the institution's         |
|    geographical operational zone.                                                      |
|                                                                                        |
| 3. ELDS (20%): Reserved for candidates from Educationally Less Developed States        |
|    (e.g., Adamawa, Bauchi, Bayelsa, Benue, Borno, Ebonyi, Jigawa, Kano, Katsina,       |
|    Kebbi, Kogi, Kwara, Nasarawa, Niger, Yobe, Zamfara).                                |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
</div>

<p>A candidate from Anambra State applying to UNILAG falls under the Merit or Open Competitive quota (since UNILAG's catchment area includes Lagos, Ogun, Osun, Oyo, Ondo, and Ekiti). If this candidate does not secure a spot within the top 45% merit quota, their status will display "Not Admitted," even if candidates with lower scores from catchment or ELDS states secure admission offers.</p>

<h3>2.5 Behind-the-Scenes: Institutional Delays & JAMB Verification</h3>
<p>Universities do not process all admission batches at once. The screening timeline usually follows a structured sequence:</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Stage 1: Primary Verification</strong> – Deans and Departmental Heads compile candidate files based on post-UTME results.</li>
<li><strong>Stage 2: Committee Approval</strong> – The Central Admissions Committee evaluates candidate files against regulatory quotas.</li>
<li><strong>Stage 3: Upload to CAPS</strong> – The university ICT desk officer uploads recommended candidates to the JAMB portal.</li>
<li><strong>Stage 4: JAMB Desk Officer Audit</strong> – JAMB officials verify candidate age (minimum 16 years), O'Level authenticity, UTME subject alignment, and total quota limits.</li>
<li><strong>Stage 5: Final System Approval</strong> – The JAMB Registrar approves the offer, changing the status from "Admission in Progress" to "Congratulations."</li>
</ol>

<p>If your institution is still reviewing candidates in Stage 1 or 2, your dashboard will display "Not Admitted" simply because processing is still ongoing.</p>

<h3>2.6 What If You Met All Requirements But Still Show "Not Admitted"?</h3>
<p>If your aggregate score exceeds the published departmental threshold and your O'Level qualifications are valid, your profile may still show "Not Admitted" due to technical issues:</p>

<ul>
<li><strong>Missing O'Level Upload on CAPS:</strong> Your institution may have received your physical screening documents, but your credentials are not linked to your central JAMB record.</li>
<li><strong>Awaiting Results (AR) Flag:</strong> You registered for UTME using an "Awaiting Result" status and failed to update your profile with cleared WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB grades.</li>
<li><strong>Age Limitation:</strong> You were under 16 years of age at the operational cutoff date set by the matriculation board.</li>
<li><strong>First Choice Policy Mismatch:</strong> The university only considers candidates who selected them as their primary choice during UTME registration.</li>
<li><strong>Incomplete Post-UTME Registration:</strong> Biometric authentication or online screening submission errors unlinked your result from the primary candidate database.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3.1 Initial Verification: Double-Checking Your Details</h3>
<p>Do not assume your credentials uploaded successfully during UTME registration. You must verify your O'Level submissions manually on the official portal.</p>

<h4>Steps to Verify O'Level Upload on JAMB CAPS (Desktop/Mobile):</h4>
<ol>
<li>Launch your mobile browser (Google Chrome or Mozilla Firefox).</li>
<li>Set your browser view settings to <strong>"Desktop Site Mode"</strong> (Crucial step: mobile views often hide CAPS options).</li>
<li>Visit the official portal at <code>efacility.jamb.gov.ng</code>.</li>
<li>Log in using your registered email address and password.</li>
<li>Select <strong>"Check Admission Status"</strong>.</li>
<li>Click <strong>"Access my CAPS"</strong>.</li>
<li>Click on <strong>"My O'Level"</strong> on the left navigation menu.</li>
<li>Confirm whether your O'Level grades (A1-C6) are fully displayed.</li>
<li><strong>If the page reads "No Result Found" or displays AR (Awaiting Result):</strong> Visit an accredited JAMB CBT Centre immediately to upload your verified WAEC/NECO statement of results. Your status will not change to "Admission in Progress" until this update reflects.</li>
</ol>

<h3>3.2 Frequent Monitoring: When and How Often to Check CAPS</h3>
<p>Avoid checking your portal every thirty minutes. Admissions update in batch runs, usually late at night or following official university senate approvals.</p>

<ul>
<li>Check your portal <strong>twice a week</strong> during active admission cycles.</li>
<li>Monitor official announcements from your institution regarding batch releases (Merit List, Catchment List, Supplementary List).</li>
<li>Check your registered SMS inbox and primary email address daily. JAMB sends notification alerts when an offer is issued or when a transfer request is generated.</li>
</ul>

<h3>3.3 Proactive Communication: Contacting Your Institution</h3>

<h4>3.3.1 Whom to Contact</h4>
<p>When your screening metrics meet admission requirements but your status remains "Not Admitted," contact the following officials:</p>
<ul>
<li>The Tertiary Institution Admissions Office (Academic Affairs Division).</li>
<li>The Faculty/Departmental Admission Desk Officer.</li>
<li>The Student Affairs ICT Liaison Desk.</li>
</ul>

<h4>3.3.2 What Information to Provide</h4>
<p>When contacting your institution, bring or attach the following documentation:</p>
<ul>
<li>Full Name (as printed on your UTME registration slip).</li>
<li>JAMB Registration Number.</li>
<li>Original UTME Score Result Notification Slip.</li>
<li>Institutional Post-UTME Screening Result Slip.</li>
<li>Verified O'Level Statement of Result (WAEC/NECO/NABTEB).</li>
<li>State of Origin Certificate (for Catchment/ELDS verification).</li>
</ul>

<h3>3.5 What to Do If Your Institution Claims No Admission Yet</h3>
<p>If your institution asserts that admission lists have not been released, but other candidates are sharing screenshots of "Admission in Progress" statuses on CAPS, understand that updates occur in rolling releases.</p>

<ol>
<li>Confirm if the candidates receiving offers applied via special quotas (Direct Entry, Sports Quota, Staff Quota, or Merit).</li>
<li>Verify if the offers issued belong to a specific department or faculty that completed screening earlier than yours.</li>
<li>Continue checking your portal twice a week. Departmental lists move through the senate review process at different speeds.</li>
</ol>

<h2>4. Troubleshooting Common "Not Admitted" Scenarios</h2>

<h3>4.1 Scenario: Your Friend Was Admitted, But You Weren't</h3>
<p><em>Context:</em> You and your classmate applied for Law at the same federal university. You scored 275 in UTME, while your classmate scored 262. Your classmate receives an admission offer on CAPS while your status remains "Not Admitted."</p>

<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                         SCENARIO ANALYSIS: CANDIDATE COMPARISON                    |
+------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| PARAMETER             | CANDIDATE A (YOU)          | CANDIDATE B (FRIEND)          |
+-----------------------+----------------------------+-------------------------------+
| UTME Score            | 275                        | 262                           |
| Post-UTME Score       | 60/100                     | 74/100                        |
| O'Level Grades        | 3 B3s, 2 C4s (15 points)   | 5 A1s (20 points)             |
| State of Origin       | Anambra (Merit Only)       | Osun (Catchment State)        |
| Calculated Aggregate  | 66.8%                      | 70.2%                         |
| STATUS                | "NOT ADMITTED"             | "CONGRATULATIONS"             |
+-----------------------+----------------------------+-------------------------------+
</div>

<h4>Strategic Steps:</h4>
<ol>
<li><strong>Recalculate total aggregate scores:</strong> UTME performance represents only part of your final assessment. Factor in Post-UTME and O'Level points.</li>
<li><strong>Verify catchment rules:</strong> Check if your classmate benefited from catchment area or ELDS quotas.</li>
<li><strong>Check O'Level sitting count:</strong> Some institutions penalize candidate aggregate scores by 5% to 10% if qualifications span two sittings.</li>
</ol>

<h3>4.2 Scenario: The Admission Deadline Has Passed</h3>
<p><em>Context:</em> The institution announces the closure of its matriculation cycle, but your JAMB CAPS status still reads "Not Admitted."</p>

<h4>Strategic Steps:</h4>
<ol>
<li><strong>Check for Supplementary List processing:</strong> Tertiary institutions frequently compile supplementary, late-clearing, or change-of-course lists after official announcements.</li>
<li><strong>Access the CAPS "Marketplace" Tab:</strong> Open your CAPS dashboard, click <strong>"Marketplace"</strong>, and check if other institutions have requested to consider your profile for alternative programs.</li>
<li><strong>Evaluate Transfer Approvals:</strong> Check if your primary choice institution transferred your profile to another department (e.g., moving an applicant from Medicine to Anatomy).</li>
</ol>

<h3>4.3 Scenario: You Saw "Admission in Progress" Previously, Now it's "Not Admitted"</h3>
<p><em>Context:</em> Your status updated to "Admission in Progress" (AIP) three weeks ago, but reverted back to "Not Admitted" today.</p>

<h4>Causes:</h4>
<ul>
<li><strong>Quota Limit Reached:</strong> The institution submitted more names than their approved regulatory cap, prompting JAMB officers to return unassigned profiles.</li>
<li><strong>O'Level Discrepancy:</strong> JAMB desk officers flagged an issue with your uploaded O'Level result during final verification (e.g., missing credit in English or Mathematics).</li>
<li><strong>Age Restriction:</strong> The candidate had not reached the minimum age requirement (16 years) by the system deadline.</li>
</ul>

<h4>Strategic Steps:</h4>
<ol>
<li>Visit an accredited JAMB CBT Centre to verify that your uploaded O'Level statement matches your original certificate details exactly.</li>
<li>Contact your university ICT/Admissions desk immediately with your registration details to request clarification regarding the system reversal.</li>
</ol>

<h3>5.1 Understanding the "Transfer Approval", "Buy", or "Not Qualified" Status</h3>

<h4>Transfer Approval</h4>
<p>If your aggregate score does not meet the merit threshold for highly competitive courses like Nursing or Law, your institution may offer you a related program (e.g., Public Health or History).</p>

<p>This offer appears under the <strong>"Transfer Approval"</strong> tab on your CAPS dashboard. You must click <strong>"Accept"</strong> to allow the university to process your admission under the new department. If you decline, your status reverts to "Not Admitted" for your original choice.</p>

<h4>CAPS Marketplace</h4>
<p>The JAMB Marketplace is an automated exchange feature where institutions with unfilled admission quotas search for qualified candidates who missed admission in high-demand universities.</p>

<p>If an institution requests your profile via Marketplace:</p>
<ol>
<li>Log into CAPS and select <strong>"Marketplace"</strong>.</li>
<li>Review the institution, proposed course, and tuition parameters.</li>
<li>Click <strong>"Accept"</strong> to grant the new institution access to your profile, or <strong>"Reject"</strong> to decline the request.</li>
</ol>

<h3>5.2 Considering a Change of Course or Institution on CAPS</h3>

<h4>5.2.1 Step-by-Step Process for Changing Course/Institution</h4>
<ol>
<li>Visit an accredited JAMB CBT Centre (Course changes cannot be completed on personal mobile devices).</li>
<li>Pay the official JAMB processing fee.</li>
<li>Select your new target course or preferred institution based on published baseline cut-off marks.</li>
<li>Verify that your UTME subject combination matches the requirements of your new chosen course.</li>
<li>Submit your request and print the updated JAMB Change of Course Slip.</li>
<li>Log into your new target university's portal to complete any required supplementary screening registration.</li>
</ol>

<section id="7-frequently-asked-questions-faq-about-not-admitted">
<h2>7. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) About "Not Admitted"</h2>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Q1: How long does the "Not Admitted" status last on JAMB CAPS?</div>
<p>The "Not Admitted" status remains visible on your dashboard from the moment your profile is generated until an institution uploads a recommendation and JAMB approves it. This process can take several months, running throughout the active admission cycle until your university completes its batch releases (Merit, Catchment, ELDS, Supplementary).</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Q2: Can JAMB CAPS status change from "Not Admitted" directly to "Congratulations"?</div>
<p>Yes. While profiles typically transition through "Admission in Progress" or "Recommended," systems often update directly from "Not Admitted" to "Congratulations" if JAMB clears your institution's batch recommendation immediately.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Q3: What should I do if my university releases its final list, but my CAPS status still says "Not Admitted"?</div>
<p>First, confirm if the published list is the final supplementary batch. If the university concludes all admission runs and your status remains "Not Admitted," explore alternative options immediately. Check the CAPS Marketplace for transfer opportunities, register for Direct Entry pathways like JUPEB or IJMB, or apply for change of course options at institutions with open quotas.</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Q4: Does clicking "DECLINE" on a transfer offer affect my primary choice?</div>
<p>If your primary choice institution officially transferred your profile to a different course, declining that transfer offer means you choose not to accept the new department. However, it does not restore your application to your original choice if the initial department has already filled its quota. Your status will revert to "Not Admitted."</p>
</div>

<div class="faq-item">
<div class="faq-question">Q5: Why are candidates with lower UTME scores being admitted while my status remains "Not Admitted"?</div>
<p>Admissions depend on your total combined aggregate score—not your UTME performance alone. Candidates with lower UTME scores may secure higher aggregate totals through better Post-UTME performance or maximum O'Level points. Additionally, candidates may qualify under Catchment Area or ELDS (Educationally Less Developed States) quotas assigned to their state of origin.</p>
</div>
</section>

<h2>8. Legal, Policy Recourse & Official JAMB Escalation Pathways</h2>

<p>If you believe your profile was wrongfully excluded from admission despite meeting all published criteria (merit cut-off score, verified O'Level combination, correct UTME subjects, age requirement), you have official administrative avenues for recourse:</p>

<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">NOTE</strong>
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                    OFFICIAL COMPLAINT ESCALATION FLOW                         |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  1. LOG TICKET ON JAMB SUPPORT PORTAL (jamb.gov.ng/support)                   |
|                                |                                              |
|                                v                                              |
|  2. SUBMIT FORMAL LETTER TO UNIVERSITY ADMISSION COMMITTEE / REGISTRAR        |
|                                |                                              |
|                                v                                              |
|  3. VISIT JAMB STATE ZONAL OFFICE FOR ADMINISTRATIVE AUDIT                    |
+-------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
</div>

<h3>Step 1: Submit an Official Ticket via the JAMB Ticketing System</h3>
<p>JAMB does not resolve individual admission disputes through social media. You must log an official ticket through the support portal:</p>
<ol>
<li>Visit <code>jamb.gov.ng/support</code>.</li>
<li>Select <strong>"Create Support Ticket"</strong>.</li>
<li>Choose <strong>"Admissions Issue"</strong> under the category drop-down menu.</li>
<li>Attach your Post-UTME result slip, UTME result notification, O'Level verification printout, and official departmental cut-off mark documentation.</li>
<li>Retain your assigned Ticket ID for tracking.</li>
</ol>

<h3>Step 2: Administrative Appeal to the University Registrar</h3>
<p>Write a formal, documented letter of appeal addressed to the Secretary of the Central Admissions Committee (The Registrar). Attach printouts of your screening scores, official cut-off publication slips, and JAMB registration confirmation details. Deliver the letter directly to the University Academic Affairs Division.</p>
