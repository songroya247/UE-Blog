<section id="mission">
  <h2>Our mission</h2>
  <p>Ultimate Edge Blog exists to make Nigerian tertiary admissions less confusing. JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and individual university portals each publish information through different channels, on different timelines, using different terminology — and a single missed detail (a wrong portal, an expired deadline, a misunderstood status) can genuinely cost a candidate their admission for the year. We write clear, practical, step-by-step guides so students and parents can navigate this process with confidence.</p>
</section>

<section id="who-we-are">
  <h2>Who we are</h2>
  <p>Ultimate Edge Blog is published by <b>Solomon Obafemi</b>, founder of UltimateEdge, an education and content brand serving Nigerian students preparing for JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and Post-UTME. Solomon also built and runs UE School, an exam-prep platform for the same audience.</p>
  <p>Individual guest authors are credited by name where they contribute a specific article; otherwise, articles are published under Solomon's byline as the site's founder and editor.</p>
</section>

<section id="approach">
  <h2>How we research and write</h2>
  <p>Every article on this site is built from official sources — JAMB circulars and portal documentation, WAEC and NECO public guidance, and individual university admissions offices — cross-checked against multiple independent sources where official channels are unclear or slow to update.</p>
  <div class="callout tip">
    <strong class="label">AI-Assisted Writing</strong>
    Some articles are drafted with the help of AI as a research and writing tool. Every piece is reviewed, fact-checked, edited, and approved by a named author before publication — AI drafts a starting point, it does not publish unsupervised.
  </div>
  <p>Admissions rules, deadlines, and portal details change from cycle to cycle. Where something in an older article may have changed, we welcome corrections — see the comment section on any article, or reach out directly using the contact details below.</p>
</section>
