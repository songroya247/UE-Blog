<section id="what-is-delsu-portal">
  <h2>What is the DELSU Admission Portal?</h2>
  <blockquote class="def">
    <b>DELSU Admission Portal</b> is the centralized, web-based application and admissions management system used by Delta State University, Abraka, to process prospective student applications, manage document verification, collect processing fees, and publish admission lists for both UTME and Direct Entry candidates.
  </blockquote>
  <p>Delta State University, Abraka, operates under the strict regulatory oversight of the National Universities Commission (NUC). The university mandates that all admission seekers route their applications through this dedicated digital infrastructure. This system eliminates physical bottlenecks, reduces administrative errors, and ensures transparent, merit-based allocations aligned with JAMB CAPS (Central Admissions Processing System) protocols.</p>
  <p>Prospective students use this portal to purchase application forms, input biographical data, upload O'Level results, select faculties and departments, and track their screening progress. The portal serves as the single source of truth for your admission journey at DELSU. No physical submission of application forms is accepted unless explicitly stated in rare, extraordinary circumstances announced by the university management.</p>

  <h2>Accessing the DELSU Admission Portal</h2>
  <p>Getting into the system requires precise navigation. The university occasionally updates its subdomain structures. Following the correct path prevents phishing attempts and ensures your data reaches the official university servers.</p>
  
  <h3>Direct Login Link & Official Website</h3>
  <p>The primary gateway for all admission-related activities is the official DELSU admissions subdomain. Do not use third-party links shared on unverified social media platforms.</p>
  <ul class="checklist">

  <h3>Account Registration (First-Time Users)</h3>
  <p>New applicants must create a profile before accessing the application form. This process binds your identity to a unique application ID.</p>
  <ol class="steps">

    <li><b>Initiate Registration:</b> Click on the "Create New Account" or "Register" button prominently displayed on the homepage.</li>

    <li><b>Purchase Application Form:</b> After verifying your email, log in and select "Purchase Application Form." You will be redirected to a secure payment gateway (typically Remita or a designated bank portal) to pay the non-refundable application fee.</li>
    <li><b>Retrieve Application ID:</b> Upon successful payment, the system generates a unique Application ID and a password. Save these credentials immediately. You will need them for all subsequent logins.</li>

<section id="step-by-step-application">
  <h2>Step-by-Step Application Process via the Portal</h2>
  <p>Executing a flawless application requires methodical preparation. Rushing through the form leads to disqualification due to simple data entry errors. Follow this exhaustive workflow.</p>

  <h3>Pre-Application Checklist</h3>
  <p>Gather these items before logging in. Having them ready prevents session timeouts and ensures accurate data entry.</p>
  <ul class="checklist">
    <li><b>Valid Email Address & Phone Number:</b> Must be active and accessible throughout the admission cycle.</li>
    <li><b>JAMB Registration Number:</b> Double-check this 10-digit number. A single wrong digit invalidates your application.</li>
    <li><b>O'Level Examination Details:</b> WAEC/NECO/NABTEB center number, year of examination, and examination number.</li>
    <li><b>Scanned Documents:</b> Passport photo, O'Level result, JAMB slip, and indigene letter, formatted to the specified sizes.</li>
    <li><b>Payment Card:</b> A debit card with active internet banking capabilities for form purchase and screening fee payment.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>Completing Your Personal Profile</h3>
  <p>Accuracy here is non-negotiable. The names you enter must match your JAMB registration and O'Level certificates exactly. Discrepancies in surname, first name, or date of birth will trigger an automatic flag during the verification stage. Enter your state of origin and local government area precisely as they appear on your official documents.</p>

  <h3>Selecting Courses and Programs</h3>
  <p>The portal allows you to select your first-choice and second-choice departments. Choose wisely. Your O'Level subject combination and JAMB subjects must align perfectly with the chosen department's requirements. For instance, applying for Nursing Science requires Credit passes in English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Biology. Selecting an incompatible course combination results in immediate disqualification, regardless of your aggregate score.</p>

  <h3>Submitting O'Level Results & JAMB Details</h3>
  <p>You will be prompted to input your O'Level subjects and grades manually, even if you upload the scanned result. The system cross-references your manual entry with the uploaded scan. Ensure you select the correct examination body (WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB) and the correct sitting (one or two sittings, depending on the department's policy). Enter your JAMB UTME score exactly as it appears on your result slip.</p>

  <h3>Uploading Supporting Documents</h3>
  <p>Navigate to the upload section. Click "Choose File" for each required document. Select the pre-formatted file from your device. Click "Upload." Wait for the green success checkmark before proceeding to the next document. Do not refresh the page during an active upload, as this corrupts the file transfer.</p>

  <h3>Making the Application Fee Payment</h3>
  <p>After completing the form, navigate to the payment section. The system will generate a unique transaction reference. Click "Pay Now" to be redirected to the secure payment gateway. Enter your card details or copy the RRR to pay via your bank's mobile app. Upon successful payment, return to the portal and click "Verify Payment." Print the payment receipt immediately.</p>

  <h3>Final Application Submission</h3>
  <p>Review every single field. Once you click the "Final Submit" button, the form locks. You cannot edit your course of study or personal data after this point without paying a hefty "Change of Course/Data" fee and undergoing a manual approval process. After submission, download and print your application summary slip. Keep this document safe; you will need it for physical screening if required.</p>
</section>

<section id="post-submission">
  <h2>Post-Submission: What to Expect and Next Steps</h2>
  <p>Submitting your form is not the end of the process. The period between submission and final admission requires active monitoring and preparation.</p>

  <h3>Checking Your Application Status Regularly</h3>
  <p>Log in to the portal at least once a week. The university updates the "Application Status" tab in batches. Look for status changes from "Submitted" to "Screening in Progress" or "Admitted." Do not rely on third-party rumors. The portal is the only official source of your status.</p>

  <h3>Admission Screening Process</h3>
  <p>DELSU conducts a rigorous internal screening process. This involves verifying your uploaded O'Level results against the NUC database and calculating your aggregate score. The aggregate score typically combines your JAMB UTME score (weighted) and your O'Level grades (weighted). For Direct Entry candidates, your ND, HND, or NCE grades are evaluated. The university may publish a list of candidates shortlisted for physical screening or departmental interviews on the portal. If your name appears, you must appear with your original documents.</p>

  <h3>Acceptance of Offer and Registration</h3>
  <p>When your status changes to "Admitted," a new module unlocks on the portal: "Accept Admission." You will be required to pay a non-refundable acceptance fee via the Remita gateway. Failure to pay this fee within the stipulated deadline (usually two to four weeks) results in the forfeiture of your admission slot. After paying the acceptance fee, you will gain access to the school fees payment portal and the course registration module for the new academic session.</p>
  <div class="callout warn">
    <strong class="label">URGENT DEADLINE WARNING</strong>
    Admission slots are strictly time-bound. If you are offered admission, you must accept and pay the acceptance fee before the portal closes the acceptance window. Late payments are automatically rejected by the system, and the slot is offered to the next candidate on the waiting list.
  </div>
</section>

<section id="troubleshooting">
  <h2>Troubleshooting Common DELSU Admission Portal Issues</h2>
  <p>Technical glitches occur. Knowing how to resolve them quickly prevents unnecessary panic and missed deadlines.</p>

  <h3>Login Problems</h3>
  <p><b>"Invalid Credentials" Error:</b> This usually means a typo in your Application ID or password. Check for leading or trailing spaces. Ensure Caps Lock is off. If you have forgotten your password, use the "Forgot Password" link. The system will send a reset link to the email address you used during registration. Check your spam folder if the email does not appear in your inbox within five minutes.</p>
  <p><b>Account Locked:</b> After five consecutive failed login attempts, the portal temporarily locks your account for security reasons. Wait 30 minutes before trying again. If the issue persists, contact the admissions support desk with your Application ID and a valid ID card for manual unlocking.</p>

  <h3>Application Submission Errors</h3>
  <p><b>"Save Error" or "Submission Failed":</b> This often happens due to unstable internet connectivity or browser cache conflicts. Clear your browser cache and cookies. Switch to a different browser (e.g., from Chrome to Firefox). Ensure all mandatory fields (marked with a red asterisk) are filled. Attempt submission during off-peak hours (early morning or late night) when server traffic is low.</p>

  <h3>Document Upload Failures</h3>
  <p><b>File Size Exceeded:</b> The portal strictly enforces file size limits. If your scanned passport is 1.5MB, it will be rejected. Use free online compression tools to reduce the file size to under 200KB without losing legibility. <b>Corrupted File Error:</b> Ensure your file is not password-protected. Save your documents as standard PDF or high-quality JPG files. Do not use obscure file formats like .HEIC or .WEBP.</p>

  <h3>Payment Transaction Issues</h3>
  <p><b>Payment Not Reflecting:</b> If your bank account was debited but the portal still shows "Unpaid," do not pay again. Wait for at least 2 to 24 hours. Bank-to-portal synchronization can experience delays. If the payment does not reflect after 24 hours, locate the transaction reference number or RRR from your bank statement. Contact the DELSU ICT support or Remita support with this reference number to trace and reconcile the payment.</p>
</section>

  <h3>JAMB UTME Requirements</h3>
  <p>Candidates must sit for the UTME and select Delta State University, Abraka as their first choice. The general JAMB cut-off mark is set by the university management, but competitive departments require significantly higher scores. A score of 200 is typically the absolute minimum for consideration, but Medicine, Law, and Nursing often require 250 and above.</p>

  <h3>Direct Entry Requirements</h3>
  <p>Direct Entry candidates must possess an Advanced Level qualification. This includes a National Diploma (ND) with a minimum of Lower Credit from an accredited polytechnic, a Higher National Diploma (HND), a Nigeria Certificate in Education (NCE) with merit, or two A-Level passes in relevant subjects. Direct Entry candidates must also register for and pass the JAMB Direct Entry screening.</p>

<section id="faq">
  <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Can I use two O'Level sittings for all courses at DELSU?</summary>
    <p>No. While many faculties accept two sittings, highly competitive programs in the College of Health Sciences (like Medicine, Surgery, and Nursing) strictly require five credit passes in a single sitting. Always verify the specific departmental requirement before applying.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>What happens if I make a mistake on my application form after submission?</summary>
    <p>Once submitted, the form is locked. You must visit the DELSU ICT center or admissions office to request a manual unlock or "Change of Data." This process attracts an administrative fee and requires physical verification of your original documents.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Is the DELSU admission form refundable if I am not admitted?</summary>
    <p>No. The application form fee and the screening fee are strictly non-refundable, regardless of the final admission outcome. These fees cover the administrative cost of processing your application.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>How is the DELSU aggregate score calculated?</summary>
    <p>The aggregate score is a weighted combination of your JAMB UTME score and your O'Level grades. Typically, the JAMB score is divided by a specific factor (e.g., 6.67 to scale it to 15%), and the O'Level grades are assigned points (e.g., A1 = 6 points, B2 = 5 points) to make up the remaining percentage. Use the <a href="https://school.ultimateedge.info">Ultimate Edge Aggregate Calculator</a> to estimate your score accurately.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Does DELSU accept awaiting results for the admission portal?</summary>
    <p>Yes, you can apply with an "Awaiting Result" status. However, you must upload your final O'Level result to the JAMB CAPS portal and the DELSU admission portal before the university begins its internal verification process. Failure to provide the result by the deadline results in automatic disqualification.</p>
  </details>
