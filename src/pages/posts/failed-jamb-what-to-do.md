<section id="understanding-your-jamb-score-failed-low-or-withheld">
  <h2>Understanding Your JAMB Score: Failed, Low, or Withheld?</h2>
  <p>Before making your next move, you must know exactly where you stand. A bad result is not a single category. Your score dictates your next logical step.</p>

  <h3>What Does "Failed" Actually Mean?</h3>
  <p>Technically, there is no "F" on a JAMB result slip. However, the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board, alongside heads of tertiary institutions, sets the <strong>National Minimum Tolerable UTME Score (NTMUS)</strong> annually.</p>

  <!-- SCORE CARDS GRID -->

  <p>If your score falls below 150, direct admission into a regular federal, state, or prominent private university via the UTME route is off the table for this year. You did not fail life—you simply fell below an administrative threshold.</p>

  <h3>Scoring "Low" vs. Failing: Navigating the Grey Area</h3>
  <p>What if you scored 185? You cleared the 150 university baseline. Yet, your dream is to study Medicine and Surgery at the University of Ibadan (UI) or Law at the University of Lagos (UNILAG).</p>
  <p>In this scenario, your score is "low" relative to your target. Elite courses at highly competitive institutions require scores far north of 250, often combined with stellar post-UTME performances.</p>

  <p>If you find yourself in this middle ground, you have two strategic options:</p>
  <ul class="styled-list">
    <li><strong>Change of Course/Institution:</strong> Purchase a JAMB data correction form for ₦2,500 (excluding CBT charges) to switch to a less competitive course or a state/private university or polytechnic.</li>
    <li><strong>The Catchment Area Strategy:</strong> Every federal university has geographical catchment areas. A score of 190 might fail at UNILAG but secure admission at a federal university in your state of origin due to lower localized cut-offs.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>The Dilemma of a Withheld or Cancelled Result</h3>
  <p>If your result returns as <em>"Result Withheld," "Under Investigation,"</em> or <em>"No Result Yet,"</em> do not panic. This usually occurs due to systemic malpractice audits, fingerprint biometric errors, or technical CBT outages.</p>

  <h3>Analyzing Your Score Breakdown</h3>
  <p>Examine individual subjects rather than just the total. For instance, a total of 170 with <strong>Physics at 15</strong> indicates that 60% of your future study time must be spent strengthening core Physics fundamentals.</p>

<section id="option-1-re-sitting-jamb-your-second-chance-strategy">
  <h2>Option 1: Re-sitting JAMB – Your Second Chance Strategy</h2>
  <p>Retaking UTME is valid if you are young (under 17), pursuing highly regulated professional degrees (Medicine, Law, Pharmacy), or simply underprepared during your first attempt.</p>

  <div class="grid-2col">
    <div class="info-card">
      <h4>🧠 Active Recall & Spaced Repetition</h4>
      <p>Do not read textbooks like novels. Test yourself with blank sheets after reading, and use spaced review cycles (Day 1, 3, 7, 30) using flashcards like Anki.</p>
    </div>
    <div class="info-card">
      <h4>🧮 Exam Preparation Tactics</h4>
      <p>Practice mental math without scientific calculators (only basic on-screen calculators are provided). For Arts, read recommended literature texts line-by-line.</p>
    </div>
  </div>
</section>

<section id="option-2-exploring-alternative-pathways-to-higher-education">
  <h2>Option 2: Exploring Alternative Pathways to Higher Education</h2>
  <p>You do not need JAMB to get into a Nigerian university. This is the best-kept secret that many parents miss.</p>

  <h3>1. Advanced Level Programs (IJMB & JUPEB)</h3>
  <p>9-month NUC-approved A-Level programs that lead directly into <strong>200 Level Direct Entry (DE)</strong>.</p>
  
  <ul class="styled-list">
    <li><strong>IJMB (Ahmadu Bello University):</strong> Popular nationwide and in Northern/Middle-Belt regions. Form: ~₦10,000 | Tuition: ₦150k – ₦350k.</li>
    <li><strong>JUPEB (University of Lagos):</strong> Strongly preferred by Southern universities (UNILAG, OAU). Form: ₦15k – ₦35k | Tuition: ₦250k – ₦800k.</li>
  </ul>

  <h3>2. Polytechnic ND to University Direct Entry</h3>
  <p>Complete a 2-year National Diploma (ND) program. With an Upper Credit, you can bypass HND and gain Direct Entry admission straight into 200 Level at participating universities.</p>

  <h3>3. Remedial / Pre-Degree Programs</h3>
  <p>Internal 1-year university prep programs that guarantee 100 Level admission upon passing internal exams—bypassing the 2-million candidate JAMB pool.</p>
</section>
