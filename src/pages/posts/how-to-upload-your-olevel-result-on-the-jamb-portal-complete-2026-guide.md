<section id="understanding-the-importance-of-uploading-your-olevel-results">
<h2>UNDERSTANDING THE IMPORTANCE OF UPLOADING YOUR O'LEVEL RESULTS</h2>

<h3>Why JAMB Requires Your O'Level Results</h3>
<p>Passing UTME is only one part of securing university placement. JAMB requires verified O'Level grades to confirm that you satisfy basic faculty entry standards. Candidates applying for competitive programs need specific credit passes in core subjects, including English Language and Mathematics. Your uploaded secondary school results provide the objective academic backing needed before any institution considers your application. Without these records in the central system, your application remains incomplete.</p>

<h3>The Role of JAMB CAPS in Admission</h3>
<p>The Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) manages candidate profiles across all tertiary institutions in Nigeria. It compares candidate scores and secondary school qualifications directly against institutional criteria. Admissions officers rely on CAPS to clear candidates for prospective lists. Candidates without verified O'Level results are generally not considered for admission until their records are available on the platform.</p>

<h3>Consequences of Not Uploading or Uploading Incorrectly</h3>
<p>Leaving your status marked as Awaiting Result after your examination board releases results delays institutional screening. Selection panels move forward with candidates whose files are complete. In addition, providing incorrect examination details may delay admission or lead to investigation and possible disqualification if records fail verification against examination board databases.</p>
</section>
<section id="prerequisites-before-visiting-a-cbt-centre">
<h2>PREREQUISITES BEFORE VISITING A CBT CENTRE</h2>

<h3>Have Your Details Ready</h3>
<p>Locate your ten-digit JAMB registration number and profile code before leaving home. Keep your eFacility login email and password handy. You will need these credentials to verify your profile once the upload process ends.</p>

<h3>Gather Your O'Level Examination Details</h3>
<p>Bring a clean printout or original statement of results from WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB. Write down your candidate examination number, examination year, centre number, and exact subject grades on a separate notepad. Double-checking these details prevents input mistakes during operator entry.</p>

<h3>Accepted Examination Bodies</h3>
<p>JAMB accepts results from three main examination bodies: West African Examinations Council (WAEC), National Examinations Council (NECO), and National Business and Technical Examinations Board (NABTEB). Both internal school exams and external GCE sittings are accepted. If you choose to combine two sittings, bring the complete details for both examination sittings to the operator.</p>
</section>

<h3>Where Uploads Take Place</h3>
<p>Candidates cannot upload O'Level results themselves through the JAMB eFacility or CAPS portal. Official policy mandates that all initial uploads and updates take place at an accredited JAMB CBT Centre or a JAMB State Office. This measure ensures data integrity and prevents fraudulent entries.</p>

<h3>Working with the CBT Operator</h3>
<p>When you arrive at an accredited centre, hand your result printout and registration details to the assigned operator. The operator logs into JAMB's administrative portal to enter your data.</p>

<ol>
<li><strong>Selecting the Correct Examination Body:</strong> The operator selects your exact exam body—WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB—from the administrative menu.</li>
<li><strong>Inputting Your Exam Number:</strong> The operator enters your ten-digit examination number. Accuracy here is vital to ensure your profile links to your actual records.</li>
<li><strong>Specifying the Year of Examination:</strong> The operator selects the calendar year you sat for the examination.</li>
<li><strong>Inputting Subjects and Grades:</strong> The operator inputs each subject line by line, selecting exact letter grades from A1 down to F9. If you are combining WAEC and NECO, the operator enters details for both sittings during the same session.</li>
<li><strong>Reviewing the Data Screen:</strong> Look at the operator's monitor before they finalize submission. Confirm that your name, candidate number, subjects, and grades match your paper slip exactly.</li>
<li><strong>Final Submission and Acknowledgment Slip:</strong> Once you confirm the details, the operator submits the entry through the administrative portal. Pay the official service fee, which ranges between ₦500 and ₦1,000 depending on the centre. Request your printed upload acknowledgment slip before leaving. This slip serves as official proof of processing.</li>
</ol>
</section>
<section id="what-to-do-after-uploading-your-result">
<h2>WHAT TO DO AFTER UPLOADING YOUR RESULT</h2>

<ol>
<li><strong>Collect and Keep Your Slip:</strong> Store your printed acknowledgment slip safely. It contains transaction details that prove you completed the upload process at an accredited venue.</li>
<li><strong>Allow Time for Server Synchronization:</strong> System processing is not always instant. JAMB CAPS usually takes between 24 and 72 hours to synchronize data from administrative uploads. Avoid panicking if your personal dashboard does not reflect updates immediately.</li>
<li><strong>Monitor Your Portal:</strong> Log into your eFacility account after two or three days to confirm that your subject breakdown appears correctly on CAPS.</li>
<li><strong>Address Missing Data Promptly:</strong> If your status still reads Awaiting Result after four days, return to the CBT centre with your acknowledgment slip so the operator can review your transaction log.</li>
</ol>
</section>
<section id="how-to-confirm-your-olevel-result-has-been-uploaded">
<h2>HOW TO CONFIRM YOUR O'LEVEL RESULT HAS BEEN UPLOADED</h2>

<p>Candidates verify their uploaded records directly on their mobile phones or computers.</p>

<p><strong>Step 1: Access the eFacility Portal</strong><br>Open your web browser and navigate to efacility.jamb.gov.ng.</p>

<figure>
  <img src="/images/candidates-portal.jpg" alt="" loading="lazy" width="800" height="450">
  <figcaption></figcaption>
</figure>

<p><strong>Step 2: Log In</strong><br>Enter your registered email address and password to open your personal dashboard.</p>

<p><strong>Step 3: Enable Desktop View on Mobile Browsers</strong><br>If you are using a smartphone, open Chrome or Safari settings and select Desktop Site. This step ensures that all CAPS menu options render properly on smaller screens.</p>

<p><strong>Step 4: Navigate to CAPS</strong><br>Click on Check Admission Status on your main dashboard. Select your examination year, enter your registration number, and click Access my CAPS.</p>
<div class="visual-aid">[Screenshot: CAPS dashboard with My O'Level tab highlighted on left menu]</div>

<p><strong>Step 5: Check the O'Level Section</strong><br>Click on My O'Level Result on the left side menu.</p>
<div class="visual-aid">[Screenshot: O'Level result display screen on CAPS showing verified grades and subject list]</div>

<p><strong>Step 6: Verify Your Information</strong><br>Check the displayed list carefully. Ensure that:
<ul>
<li>The examination body matches your exam slip.</li>
<li>The examination year is correct.</li>
<li>Every subject and grade letter matches your statement of result.</li>
</ul>
<p>If your grades are clearly listed, your JAMB CAPS O'Level verification is complete.</p>
</section>
<section id="troubleshooting-common-uploading-issues">
<h2>TROUBLESHOOTING COMMON UPLOADING ISSUES</h2>

<div class="trouble-card">
<h3>Awaiting Result Still Showing After Upload</h3>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Database sync delay between administrative servers and CAPS, or network latency.</p>
<p><strong>Solution:</strong> Wait 24 to 72 hours. Clear browser cache or use an incognito browser window. If the issue persists past 4 days, take your acknowledgment slip back to the CBT centre to re-check submission logs.</p>
</div>

<div class="trouble-card">
<h3>Incorrect Grades Uploaded</h3>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Operator entry error during data input at the CBT centre.</p>
<p><strong>Solution:</strong> Return to an accredited CBT centre immediately. Request an update to correct the wrong grade letters. Bring your original result slip and acknowledgment slip.</p>
</div>

<div class="trouble-card">
<h3>Wrong Examination Year Selected</h3>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Miscommunication during operator processing.</p>
<p><strong>Solution:</strong> Visit an accredited centre to initiate a record modification. The operator will select the correct year and resubmit your details to the central portal.</p>
</div>

<div class="trouble-card">
<h3>Upload Completed but CAPS Says No O'Level Result</h3>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Profile mismatch or failure to submit the transaction properly at the operator workstation.</p>
<p><strong>Solution:</strong> Verify that the JAMB registration number used during upload matches your current active profile. If numbers match, ask the CBT operator to re-process the entry.</p>
</div>

<div class="trouble-card">
<h3>Combining Two Sittings Not Displaying Correctly</h3>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> Operator uploaded only one sitting or failed to check the two-sitting option on the administrative form.</p>
<p><strong>Solution:</strong> Return to the CBT centre. Ensure the operator inputs both WAEC and NECO records under the primary and secondary sitting fields before submitting.</p>
</div>

<div class="trouble-card">
<h3>Network-Related Delays During Verification</h3>
<p><strong>Cause:</strong> High user traffic on JAMB eFacility servers during peak admission periods.</p>
<p><strong>Solution:</strong> Access the portal late at night or early in the morning when server load is lower.</p>
</div>
</section>
<section id="frequently-asked-questions-faq">
<h2>FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)</h2>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I upload my result after Post-UTME registration?</summary>
<p>Yes. You can complete your JAMB O'Level upload after registering for or writing Post-UTME. However, you should complete it as soon as your secondary school results are available so institutions can evaluate your full file during admission screening.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I upload my result more than once?</summary>
<p>Yes. If you sat for a second examination to replace poor grades, or if an earlier upload contained errors, you can visit an accredited CBT centre to update your records.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I change an already uploaded result?</summary>
<p>Yes. Visiting an accredited CBT centre allows you to modify previously submitted subject grades or swap an old result for a newly released one.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I combine WAEC and NECO results?</summary>
<p>Yes. JAMB allows candidates to combine two sittings across recognized examination bodies like WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB, provided the receiving tertiary institution accepts combined results for your chosen course.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How long does it take before CAPS updates?</summary>
<p>While operator processing takes about 10 to 15 minutes at the CBT centre, portal synchronization on CAPS typically takes between 24 and 72 hours.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Is there a deadline for uploading O'Level results?</summary>
<p>JAMB does not set one single national deadline for all candidates. Instead, deadlines depend on institutional screening schedules. You should complete your upload before your target institution begins its admission selection exercise.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Does Awaiting Result affect admission?</summary>
<p>Yes. Candidates with Awaiting Result status on CAPS are generally not considered for provisional admission offers until verified grades are present in the system.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I upload NABTEB results?</summary>
<p>Yes. NABTEB results are fully recognized by JAMB for general admission and technical course placements.</p>
</details>

<details class="faq">
<summary>How much does O'Level upload cost at CBT centres?</summary>
<p>The standard service fee ranges between ₦500 and ₦1,000 at accredited CBT centres and JAMB State Offices.</p>
</details>
