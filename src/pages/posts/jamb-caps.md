## What is JAMB CAPS?

**JAMB CAPS** (Central Admissions Processing System) is the automated, web-based platform the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board uses to coordinate admissions into Nigerian tertiary institutions.

It prevents duplicate offers and is the sole legally recognized system for accepting or rejecting admission.

## Decoding Your Admission Status

Here is the real order candidates move through during the admission cycle:

* **Not Admitted / No Offer Yet:** Your school hasn't uploaded its current recommendation batch.
* **Admission in Progress (AIP):** The school has forwarded your name to JAMB.
* **Approved:** JAMB has cleared the school's recommendation.
* **Admitted:** Official confirmation! Accept or Reject options are live.

## Action Plan
1. Check CAPS weekly.
2. Verify O'Level uploads at an accredited CBT centre.
3. Accept offers within 4 weeks.
