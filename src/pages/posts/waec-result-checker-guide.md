## Introduction: What You Need to Know Before Checking Your WAEC Result

### The Importance of Your WAEC Result
The West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) is more than just a high school exit exam. It is the gatekeeper to your academic and professional future in West Africa. Whether you plan to secure admission into a university, apply for a polytechnic program, or enter the job market, this piece of paper carries immense weight.

In Nigeria, your grades must be uploaded directly to the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS). Without registering your O'Level credits on this centralized portal, tertiary institutions cannot process your admission.

### Key Information Required for Checking
To access your scores, gather these details:

- **Exam Year:** The specific calendar year you sat for the exams (e.g., 2026).
- **Exam Type:** Specify whether you wrote the school-based exam or private candidate exam.
- **Index/Examination Number:** A unique 10-digit identifier (e.g., 4250101001).
- **Result Checker PIN / Scratch Card:** A unique Personal Identification Number (PIN) and Serial Number.

## How to Check Your WASSCE for School Candidates (May/June) Results

### Step 1: Obtain Your Result Checker PIN
In many schools, WAEC includes the result checker PIN on the candidate’s smart identity card. If lost, you can buy an e-PIN online via authorized platforms like Remita, JumiaPay, or VTpass.

### Step 2: Access the Official WAECDirect Portal
Open your web browser and go to `www.waecdirect.org`.
