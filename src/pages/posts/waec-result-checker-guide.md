<section id="prerequisites">
  <h2>Prerequisites: What You Need Before Checking</h2>
  <p>The West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) is the primary gateway to tertiary education and employment across West Africa. In Nigeria, candidates must upload their passing O'Level grades directly to the JAMB Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) before higher institutions can process admission offers.</p>
  <blockquote class="def"><b>WAEC Index Number</b> is a unique 10-digit candidate identifier created by combining a 7-digit school/centre code with a 3-digit candidate seat number (for example, <code>4250101001</code>).</blockquote>
  <p>Before attempting to log into the examination database, gather all required credentials. Missing even one character will prevent the database from retrieving your record.</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>10-digit Examination Number:</strong> Your 7-digit centre code followed by your 3-digit candidate seat number.</li>
    <li><strong>Examination Year:</strong> The exact calendar year you sat for the examination (e.g., 2026).</li>
    <li><strong>Examination Type:</strong> Specify whether you registered as a school candidate or private candidate.</li>
    <li><strong>Result Checker PIN and Serial Number:</strong> A 12-digit numeric PIN and alphanumeric serial number found on your candidate identity card or purchased e-PIN receipt.</li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section id="checking-school-results">
  <h2>Checking School Candidate Results (May/June)</h2>
  <p>School candidates writing the May/June WASSCE usually receive a result checking PIN printed directly on their smart candidate identity cards. If your card is unavailable, purchase a replacement e-PIN from authorized vendors such as VTpass, Remita, or commercial banking portals.</p>
  <ol class="steps">
    <li><strong>Open the Portal:</strong> Launch your browser and navigate to <code>www.waecdirect.org</code>. Avoid third-party proxy sites that harvest login credentials.</li>
    <li><strong>Enter Index Number:</strong> Input your 10-digit Examination Number in the designated field. Confirm that no spaces exist between centre and seat numbers.</li>
    <li><strong>Select Exam Parameters:</strong> Choose your examination year and select <strong>"SCHOOL CANDIDATE RESULTS"</strong> from the Examination Type dropdown menu.</li>
    <li><strong>Enter Security Tokens:</strong> Type your alphanumeric Card Serial Number (e.g., <code>WRN123456789</code>) and 12-digit Result Checker PIN.</li>
    <li><strong>Submit Request:</strong> Click the green "Submit" button to load your full statement of results and subject breakdown.</li>
  </ol>

<section id="checking-private-results">
  <h2>Checking Private Candidate Results (GCE)</h2>
  <p>Private candidate examinations (GCE) take place across two separate windows each year: First Series (January/February) and Second Series (November/December). Unlike school candidates, private candidates must purchase result checker tokens independently.</p>
  <p>While registration for private candidates occurs on specialized registration portals (such as <code>reg.waecdirect.org</code>), result checking is handled directly on the standard <code>waecdirect.org</code> page.</p>
  <div class="callout warn">
    <strong class="label">Important Selection Rule</strong>
    When checking GCE results, ensure you select <strong>"PRIVATE CANDIDATE RESULTS"</strong> in the Examination Type menu. Choosing "School Candidate Results" for a GCE index number returns a "Candidate Does Not Exist" error.
  </div>
</section>

<section id="verification-and-digital-certificates">
  <h2>WAEC Digital Certificate & Verification</h2>
  <p>Physical paper result printouts are temporary credentials. Foreign universities, government agencies, and corporate employers require official verification or certified transcripts directly from the exam board.</p>
  <p>The official <strong>WAEC Digital Certificate Platform</strong> (<code>waec.org</code>) provides immediate digital access, downloading, and sharing for candidates who completed exams from 1999 to date.</p>

<section id="troubleshooting-errors">
  <h2>Troubleshooting Common Portal Errors</h2>
  <p>Technical glitches and typos can prevent your result page from loading. Below are the standard error codes reported on the WAECDirect portal and how to resolve them.</p>

  <h3>"Invalid Index Number"</h3>
  <p>This indicates that the entered 10-digit code does not exist in the selected database year. Check your examination slip to confirm your centre number and candidate seat number. Ensure no extra spaces or letters were included.</p>

  <h3>"Invalid PIN / Expired Scratch Card"</h3>
  <p>This message appears when the 12-digit PIN is mistyped or when the 5-check limit on the token has been exhausted. Verify that you haven't confused similar characters like <code>0</code> and <code>O</code> or <code>8</code> and <code>B</code>. If you have checked the result 5 times already, purchase a new e-PIN.</p>

  <h3>"No Result Yet" / "Result Awaiting"</h3>
  <p>This status indicates that your scripts or school Continuous Assessment (CASS) scores are undergoing final processing or moderation. Check back after a few days. If the message persists past two weeks, report the issue to your school administrator or local WAEC office.</p>

  <h3>"Candidate Does Not Exist"</h3>
  <p>This error occurs when there is a mismatch between the index number, examination year, and exam type. Double-check that a May/June exam is not set to "Private Candidate" in the dropdown selection.</p>

  <div class="callout warn">
    <strong class="label">Only trust official channels</strong>
    If web scripts on the portal time out during high-traffic periods, don't rely on random "WAEC support" emails or third-party domains found in search results — verify any support contact directly against the one listed on <code>waecdirect.org</code> itself before sending any personal details.
  </div>
</section>

<section id="faq">
  <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>How many times can I check my result with one PIN?</summary>
    <p>You can use a single result checker PIN to view a specific candidate result a maximum of 5 times. Beyond this limit, a new PIN must be purchased.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>Can I check my WAEC result online without buying a PIN?</summary>
    <p>No. The database requires a valid card PIN and serial number for security authentication. However, many secondary schools issue candidate smart identity cards containing pre-loaded PINs during exam registration.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>How long after exams are WAEC results usually released?</summary>
    <p>WAEC typically releases WASSCE results within 45 to 60 days following the completion of the final examination paper.</p>
  </details>
  <details class="faq">
    <summary>What should I do if my result is marked as withheld?</summary>
    <p>Wait for the official decision of the Nigeria Examinations Committee (NEC). If cleared after investigation, your result will be released on the portal; if found guilty of malpractice, the affected subjects will be cancelled.</p>
  </details>
</section>

<section id="action-plan">
  <h2>Next Steps and Action Plan</h2>
  <div class="action-plan">
    <h3>Post-Result Action Checklist</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Upload to JAMB CAPS:</strong> Visit an accredited CBT centre to upload your O'Level grades to the JAMB portal so tertiary institutions can process your admission.</li>
      <li><strong>Save a Digital PDF Copy:</strong> Save your statement of results as a clear PDF on secure cloud storage (Google Drive or iCloud) to prevent burning through your 5 card views.</li>
      <li><strong>Address Deficiencies Early:</strong> If key subjects fall below credit pass level (C6), register promptly for the upcoming private candidate GCE series to make up the missing credits.</li>
      <li><strong>Request Official Transcripts:</strong> Use <code>waec.org</code> to send certified digital certificates directly to tertiary institutions or overseas evaluation agencies.</li>
    </ul>
  </div>
