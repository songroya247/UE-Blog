<section id="overview">
<h2>Overview & Required Credentials</h2>
<p>The West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) serves as the core academic gateway for secondary students across West Africa. Whether you are uploading grades to the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS) in Nigeria or applying directly to tertiary institutions in Ghana, retrieving your statement of results promptly is essential.</p>
<blockquote class="def">
<b>Index Number</b> is a unique 10-digit identifier generated by combining a 7-digit school centre number with a 3-digit candidate seat number (e.g., 4250101001).
</blockquote>
<p>To successfully log into the portal or query the database via SMS, you need four key pieces of information ready before starting:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Candidate Examination Number:</strong> Your 10-digit centre and seat code.</li>
<li><strong>Examination Year:</strong> The calendar year you sat for the exam (e.g., 2026).</li>
<li><strong>Examination Type:</strong> Specify whether you sat as a School Candidate (May/June) or Private Candidate (GCE).</li>
<li><strong>Result Checker Token:</strong> A 12-digit PIN and alphanumeric Serial Number provided on smart identity cards or purchased as an e-PIN.</li>
</ul>
<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">GET YOUR PIN READY</strong>
School candidates often receive their initial result checker PIN directly on the candidate smart ID card issued during registration. If misplaced, e-PINs can be purchased via Remita, JumiaPay, VTpass, or local commercial banks.
</div>
</section>

<section id="checking-online">
<h2>How to Check Online</h2>
<p>The primary method for accessing your complete statement of results—including subject grades, candidate photograph, and performance summary—is through the official web portal.</p>
<ol class="steps">
<li><b>Obtain your Result Checker PIN:</b> Retrieve your PIN from your school smart card or purchase an e-PIN token online.</li>
<li><b>Navigate to the WAECDirect Portal:</b> Launch your web browser and open <code>www.waecdirect.org</code>.</li>
<li><b>Input Candidate Identification:</b> Type your 10-digit examination number into the designated field.</li>
<li><b>Select Exam Year and Type:</b> Choose the examination year and select "SCHOOL CANDIDATE RESULT" from the dropdown menu.</li>
<li><b>Enter PIN and Serial Details:</b> Enter your alphanumeric Card Serial Number (e.g., prefix "WRN") and 12-digit PIN.</li>
<li><b>Submit and Fetch Results:</b> Click "Submit" to load and view your official statement of results.</li>
</ol>

<section id="sms-method">
<h2>Checking WAEC Results via SMS</h2>
<p>If internet connectivity is unstable or the main website is experiencing high server traffic following result releases, candidates can instantly retrieve their grades via SMS.</p>
<p>This service is supported across major mobile networks including MTN, Airtel, and Glo.</p>
<div class="callout tip">
<strong class="label">SMS FORMAT & SHORTCODE</strong>
Open your text messaging application, type <code>WAEC*ExamNo*PIN*ExamYear</code> (without spaces), and send it to <strong>32327</strong>.
<br /><br />
<em>Example text:</em> <code>WAEC*4250101001*123456789012*2026</code>
<br /><br />
A flat rate of <strong>₦30</strong> will be charged directly from your mobile phone airtime balance.
</div>
</section>

<section id="private-candidates">
<h2>WASSCE for Private Candidates (GCE)</h2>
<p>Private candidate examinations occur in two distinct sessions: the First Series (held early in the year) and the Second Series (held during November/December). Unlike school candidates, private candidates do not receive registration smart cards from secondary schools and must purchase tokens independently.</p>
<p>Checking your GCE results follows the exact same online steps on <code>waecdirect.org</code>, with one crucial distinction: in the <strong>"Examination Type"</strong> dropdown menu, you must select <strong>"PRIVATE CANDIDATE RESULTS"</strong>.</p>
<div class="callout warn">
<strong class="label">SERIES MISMATCH ERROR</strong>
Selecting "School Candidate Results" or picking the wrong series when checking GCE grades causes the database to look up candidate records in the wrong section, resulting in a false "Candidate Does Not Exist" error.
</div>
</section>

<section id="troubleshooting">
<h2>Troubleshooting Common Errors</h2>
<p>When server traffic surges or details are miskeyed, candidates may encounter login errors on the main result portal. Below are standard error messages and their solutions:</p>
<div class="callout warn">
<strong class="label">INVALID INDEX NUMBER</strong>
Confirm that your candidate number contains exactly 10 digits without letters, spaces, or hyphens. Ensure the first 7 digits match your school centre code and the last 3 digits represent your candidate seat number.
</div>
<div class="callout warn">
<strong class="label">EXPIRED SCRATCH CARD / INVALID PIN</strong>
Single e-PINs expire after being used 5 times. If your PIN is new, double-check easily confused characters like the digit <code>0</code> versus letter <code>O</code>, or digit <code>8</code> versus letter <code>B</code>.
</div>
<div class="callout warn">
<strong class="label">CANDIDATE DOES NOT EXIST</strong>
Verify that the selected <em>Examination Year</em> and <em>Examination Type</em> exactly match your registration slip. A school candidate selecting "Private Candidate Results" will cause this error.
</div>
<p>Candidates should also understand the status designations assigned to withheld or missing subjects:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Awaiting / Outstanding:</strong> Continuous assessment scores or specific subject scripts are under final database moderation and will be released shortly.</li>
<li><strong>Pending / Withheld:</strong> The candidate or examination center is suspected of malpractice. Results are locked while the Nigeria Examinations Committee (NEC) reviews the case.</li>
<li><strong>Cancelled:</strong> Malpractice was officially proven, and the subject grades have been invalidated permanently.</li>
</ul>
</section>

<section id="faq">
<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
<details class="faq">
<summary>How many times can I check my WAEC result with one PIN?</summary>
<p>You can use a single WAEC result checker PIN to view your results a maximum of 5 times. After the fifth check, the PIN is deactivated and a new e-PIN must be purchased.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq">
<summary>Can I check my WAEC result online without a PIN?</summary>
<p>No. Accessing candidate records on the database requires a valid PIN. School candidates usually receive their initial PIN pre-printed on their registration smart ID cards.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq">
<summary>How long does WAEC take to release results after exams?</summary>
<p>WAEC releases WASSCE results within 45 to 60 days following the conclusion of the final examination paper.</p>
</details>
<details class="faq">
<summary>What should I do if my WAEC result is marked as withheld?</summary>
<p>Withheld results undergo investigation by the Nigeria Examinations Committee (NEC). If cleared, your grades will be released online; if found guilty, affected subjects will be cancelled.</p>
</details>
</section>

<div class="action-plan">
<h3>Post-Result Checklist</h3>
<ul>
<li><b>Save a digital PDF immediately:</b> Use your browser's print dialog (<code>Ctrl + P</code> or <code>Cmd + P</code>) and choose "Save as PDF" to preserve your statement without wasting card checks.</li>
<li><b>Upload to JAMB CAPS:</b> Nigerian candidates must present their O'Level credits at an accredited CBT center to update their JAMB profile for tertiary admissions.</li>
<li><b>Remediate deficient grades:</b> If core subjects (English Language or Mathematics) fall below C6 credit passes, register promptly for the upcoming private candidate GCE series.</li>
<li><b>Request digital certification for international use:</b> For overseas study or official employment verification, request certified digital dispatch through <code>waec.org</code>.</li>
</ul>
