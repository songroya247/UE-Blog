  <section id="intro">
    <h2>What You Need to Know Before Checking Your WAEC Result</h2>
    <p>The West African Senior School Certificate Examination (WASSCE) is more than a high school exit exam — it's the gatekeeper to your academic and professional future across West Africa. In Nigeria, your grades must be uploaded directly to the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) Central Admissions Processing System (CAPS); without registering your O'Level credits there, tertiary institutions can't process your admission. Ghana's public university system works the same way, pulling candidate results straight from the database.</p>
    <blockquote class="def">
      With over 1.6 million candidates writing WASSCE annually, WAEC's servers face extreme load right after results are released. Have every credential ready before you log in — a single missing digit can lock you out.
    </blockquote>
    <h3>What you'll need</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>Exam Year</b> — the calendar year you sat the exam (e.g., 2026).</li>
      <li><b>Exam Type</b> — School Candidate (May/June) or Private Candidate (GCE).</li>
      <li><b>Index/Examination Number</b> — your 10-digit identifier: a 7-digit school centre number plus your 3-digit seat number (e.g., 4250101001).</li>
      <li><b>Result Checker PIN / Scratch Card</b> — a unique PIN and Serial Number, bought physically or digitally.</li>
    </ul>
    <h3>Ways to check your result</h3>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li><b>WAECDirect Portal</b> — the primary, most detailed method for computer and smartphone users.</li>
      <li><b>SMS Service</b> — works without an internet connection.</li>
      <li><b>WAEC Mobile App</b> — for on-the-go checking and saving results.</li>
      <li><b>WAEC Digital Certificate Platform</b> — for accessing and sharing your official digital certificate.</li>
    </ol>
  </section>
  <section id="school-candidates">
    <h2>How to Check Your WASSCE for School Candidates (May/June) Results</h2>
    <p>This route is for students currently enrolled in a recognized secondary school.</p>
    <h3>Step 1 — Get your Result Checker PIN</h3>
    <p>Many schools print the result checker PIN directly on the candidate's smart identity card issued at registration. If your school didn't provide one, or you've lost it, you can buy an e-PIN through authorized platforms like Remita, JumiaPay, VTpass, or a local commercial bank. You'll receive a 12-digit PIN and an alphanumeric Serial Number by SMS or email.</p>
    <h3>Step 2 — Open the portal</h3>
    <p>Go to <code>www.waecdirect.org</code> in your browser.</p>

    <h3>Step 3 — Enter your details correctly</h3>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li>Enter your 10-digit candidate number in <b>Examination Number</b> — double-check you haven't swapped any digits.</li>
      <li>Select the year you sat the exam in <b>Examination Year</b>.</li>
      <li>Choose <b>SCHOOL CANDIDATE RESULTS</b> under <b>Examination Type</b>.</li>
    </ol>
    <h3>Step 4 — Enter your PIN and Serial Number</h3>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li>Type your Serial Number into <b>Card Serial Number</b> — it often starts with a letter prefix like "WRN."</li>
      <li>Enter the 12-digit numeric code into <b>PIN</b>, with no spaces or hyphens.</li>
    </ol>
    <h3>Step 5 — Submit and view your result</h3>
    <p>Review every field for typos, then click <b>Submit</b>. If your details match WAEC's database, your statement of results loads with your candidate photo, school name, subject list, and grades (A1 to F9).</p>

  <section id="sms">
    <h2>Alternative Method: Checking via SMS</h2>
    <p>If you don't have internet data, or the portal is slow due to server traffic, check via SMS — supported by MTN, Airtel, and Glo.</p>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li>Open your phone's messaging app.</li>
      <li>Type <code>WAEC*ExamNumber*PIN*ExamYear</code> with no spaces. Example: <code>WAEC*4250101001*123456789012*2026</code></li>
      <li>Send it to the shortcode <b>32327</b>.</li>
      <li>Wait a few minutes for an SMS with your grades.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="callout tip">
      <strong class="label">Cost</strong>
      This service carries a flat charge of ₦30, deducted directly from your mobile airtime balance.
    </div>
  </section>
  <section id="private-candidates">
    <h2>How to Check Your WASSCE for Private Candidates (GCE) Results</h2>
    <p>The WASSCE for Private Candidates (GCE) runs in two series: the First Series (around January/February) and the Second Series (around November/December). Private candidates don't receive smart cards from a school, so you'll need to buy your result checker tokens independently.</p>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li><b>Buy your e-PIN</b> through the WAEC portal or an authorized merchant.</li>
      <li><b>Visit</b> <code>www.waecdirect.org</code>.</li>
      <li><b>Enter your 10-digit</b> private candidate examination number.</li>
      <li><b>Select Exam Type</b> — choose <b>PRIVATE CANDIDATE RESULTS</b> from the dropdown. This is the step candidates get wrong most often.</li>
      <li><b>Select the exact year</b> of your examination.</li>
      <li><b>Enter your Serial Number and 12-digit PIN</b>, then submit.</li>
    </ol>
    <p>Registration for private exams happens on a specialized portal (<code>reg.waecdirect.org</code>), but checking your final result is handled on the standard <code>waecdirect.org</code> page.</p>
  </section>
  <section id="other-exams">
    <h2>Checking Other Exams</h2>
    <h3>GCE-specific notes</h3>
    <p>Because WAEC runs multiple private candidate series each year, selecting the wrong session in the dropdown triggers a "Candidate Does Not Exist" error. Always confirm whether your sitting was the First or Second Series before entering your card details.</p>
    <h3>Not a WAEC candidate?</h3>
    <p>If you wrote NECO exams, don't use the WAEC portal — check your scores on the <a href="https://results.neco.gov.ng/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">NECO Results Portal</a> using a NECO token instead. Alternative WAEC registration access is also available via <code>waeconline.org.ng</code>.</p>
  </section>
  <section id="verification">
    <h2>WAEC Result Verification: What It Is and How It's Done</h2>
    <p>A printed statement of results is rarely enough for a university application abroad or a job with a government agency — organizations need proof your scores haven't been altered. Result verification is the formal channel that lets third parties confirm authentic, unaltered records directly with WAEC.</p>
    <h3>Official verification services</h3>
    <p>WAEC's <b>Digital Certificate Platform</b> (accessible at <code>portal.waec.org</code>) lets candidates who sat exams from 1999 onward access, download, and share an official digital certificate instantly. The older physical verification route is still available through the <a href="https://waeconline.org.ng/verification" target="_blank" rel="noopener">WAEC Results Verification Portal</a>.</p>

    <h3>How to request verification</h3>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li><b>Register an account</b> at <code>waec.org</code> (or via the WAEC Digital Certificate mobile app) using your legal name, and verify your identity with your NIN or BVN.</li>
      <li><b>Retrieve your certificate</b> by entering your exam number, year, and type.</li>
      <li><b>Pay the access fee</b> — a one-time charge of <b>₦7,500</b> to unlock your certificate.</li>
      <li><b>Share it</b> with an institution by entering their email address — this costs an additional <b>₦3,500</b> per recipient.</li>
    </ol>
    <div class="callout tip">
      <strong class="label">Lost your candidate number?</strong>
      You can recover it through the same portal for a separate ₦3,000 fee, using your personal details to confirm identity.

  <section id="troubleshooting">
    <h2>Comprehensive Troubleshooting Guide</h2>
    <h3>"Invalid Index Number"</h3>
    <p><b>What it means:</b> The system doesn't recognize the 10-digit number entered.<br/><b>Fix:</b> Check your exam slip — confirm the first 7 digits match your school's centre code and the last 3 are your seat number, with no spaces or letters mixed in.</p>
    <h3>"Invalid PIN" or "Expired Scratch Card"</h3>
    <p><b>What it means:</b> The 12-digit PIN is wrong, or the card has hit its usage limit.<br/><b>Fix:</b> A single e-PIN or scratch card checks one result up to <b>5 times</b> before it's deactivated. On a new card, watch for easily confused characters like <code>8</code> vs. <code>B</code>, or <code>0</code> vs. <code>O</code>.</p>
    <h3>"No Result Yet" / "Result Awaiting"</h3>
    <p><b>What it means:</b> Your paper hasn't been fully graded or uploaded yet.<br/><b>Fix:</b> This usually means your school's Continuous Assessment (CASS) marks were uploaded late or are under final review. Check back in a few days; if it persists more than two weeks after general release, contact your school principal or the nearest WAEC office.</p>
    <h3>"Subject Combination Error"</h3>
    <p><b>What it means:</b> A mismatch between your registered subjects and what the portal shows.<br/><b>Fix:</b> This is a systemic issue you can't resolve from your browser — collect your registration slip and file a formal complaint through your secondary school.</p>
    <h3>"Candidate Does Not Exist"</h3>
    <p><b>What it means:</b> Your exam number is valid, just not for the year or exam type selected.<br/><b>Fix:</b> Recheck your Exam Type and Exam Year — selecting "Private Candidate" for a school-based sitting (or vice versa) points the system at the wrong database.</p>
    <h3>Lost your PIN?</h3>
    <p>If you bought an e-PIN online, check your email, SMS history, or banking app for the transaction receipt. If you lost a physical scratch card before viewing your result, there's no recovery option — you'll need a new e-PIN.</p>
    <h3>Missing or incorrect results</h3>
    <p>If a subject is missing entirely, or shows "Outstanding," your script is likely still under validation. School candidates should report this to their school authorities, who compile and forward affected cases to WAEC. Private candidates should contact the WAEC helpdesk or visit their state's National Office directly.</p>
    <div class="callout warn">
      <strong class="label">Only trust official channels</strong>
      For persistent portal issues, go through WAEC's official National Office channels or the technical support contact listed on your country's WAECDirect page (for Nigeria and Sierra Leone, this is currently handled via Vatebra, WAEC's technology partner). Be wary of any third-party domain or email address claiming to be "WAEC support" that you find outside the official site — verify it against the contact listed on <code>waecdirect.org</code> itself before sending any personal details.
    </div>
    <h3>Understanding result statuses</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>Awaiting</b> — marked, but held back for database sync or final moderation. Usually releases soon.</li>
      <li><b>Pending / Withheld</b> — the candidate or centre is under investigation for suspected malpractice by the Nigeria Examinations Committee (NEC).</li>
      <li><b>Cancelled</b> — malpractice was confirmed and the grade is permanently wiped; not recoverable or appealable.</li>
    </ul>

  <section id="tips">
    <h2>Tips for a Smooth Checking Experience</h2>
    <h3>Best time to check</h3>
    <p>When results first go live, millions of candidates, parents, and educators hit the portal at once, often causing slowdowns or timeouts. Try checking during off-peak hours — late night (11 PM–4 AM) or early morning.</p>
    <h3>Mobile vs. desktop</h3>
    <p>Mobile is convenient for a quick check, but desktop browsers handle the result page layout better for saving or printing — you won't risk cutting off your grade legend or candidate photo.</p>
    <h3>Connection matters</h3>
    <p>Use a standard browser (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) with a reliable connection. Heavy data-saving modes in some mobile browsers can block the background scripts the secure login form needs.</p>
    <h3>Saving and printing</h3>
    <p>Save your result the moment it loads. Never upload your credentials or result slip to public document-sharing sites like Scribd — treat your statement of results as a confidential academic document.</p>
    <ul>
      <li>On a <b>computer</b>: press <code>Ctrl+P</code> (<code>Cmd+P</code> on Mac), set the destination to <b>Save as PDF</b>.</li>
      <li>On a <b>smartphone</b>: open your browser menu, tap <b>Share</b> → <b>Print</b> → <b>Save as PDF</b>.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>For uploading to tertiary admission portals, use a clear, high-resolution PDF — a cropped image may be rejected by registration officers.</p>
  </section>
  <section id="apps">
    <h2>WAEC Result Checker Apps: Pros & Cons</h2>
    <p>WAEC provides an official mobile app on the Google Play Store — essentially a portal wrapper with the same database access as the desktop site, in a mobile-friendly interface.</p>
    <h3>Downloading the app</h3>
    <ol class="steps">
      <li>Open the Google Play Store.</li>
      <li>Search "WAEC Result Checker."</li>
      <li>Verify the developer name to avoid clone apps.</li>
      <li>Install, launch, and enter your Exam Number, Year, Type, PIN, and Serial Number.</li>
    </ol>
    <h3>App vs. website</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><b>The App:</b> Faster logins, saves credentials locally, lower data use. Printing directly from mobile can run into driver compatibility issues.</li>
      <li><b>The Website:</b> No installation needed, straightforward PDF saving and printing. Requires re-entering credentials each visit.</li>
    </ul>

  <section id="faq">
    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>
    <details class="faq">
      <summary>How many times can I check my result with one PIN?</summary>
      <p>A single result checker PIN allows up to <b>5 checks</b>. After that, the token deactivates and you'll need a new one.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq">
      <summary>Can I check my result without a PIN?</summary>
      <p>No — accessing WAEC's database requires a valid PIN. School candidates often have one pre-loaded on their registration smart card. Alternatively, the Digital Certificate platform can retrieve your official certificate for a fee, without a checker PIN.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq">
      <summary>How long does it take for results to be released?</summary>
      <p>WAEC generally releases WASSCE results within <b>45 to 60 days</b> after the final paper of the exam period.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq">
      <summary>What if my result is withheld?</summary>
      <p>Wait for the Nigeria Examinations Committee (NEC) to complete its review. If cleared, your result is released; if malpractice is confirmed, it's permanently cancelled.</p>
    </details>
    <details class="faq">
      <summary>How do I get a certified true copy of my result?</summary>
      <p>Visit the WAEC National Office in your country and apply directly, or use the WAEC Digital Certificate Platform to download and print a verifiable digital certificate.</p>
    </details>
