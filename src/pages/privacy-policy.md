<section id="what-we-collect">
  <h2>What we collect</h2>
  <p>Like virtually any website, our hosting infrastructure (Cloudflare) automatically logs standard technical information for every visit — your IP address, browser type, device type, and pages viewed — for security and performance purposes. This is infrastructure-level logging common to nearly all websites, not something we actively collect or analyze ourselves.</p>
  <p>Beyond that, we only collect information you actively choose to provide: your name and message when you post a comment, and an anonymous "like" action on an article.</p>
</section>

<section id="comments">
  <h2>Comments</h2>
  <p>You do not need to create an account or sign in to leave a comment. When you comment, we store the name you type, your comment text, and the time it was posted. We do not require or knowingly collect your email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier through the comment form.</p>
  <div class="callout warn">
    <strong class="label">Please don't share sensitive information</strong>
    Since comments are public and visible to any visitor, please don't include your JAMB registration number, phone number, email address, or other personal identifiers in a comment. Anything you type in the comment box is publicly visible.
  </div>
  <p>We reserve the right to remove any comment that is spam, abusive, or otherwise violates our <a href="/terms-of-service">Terms of Service</a>.</p>
</section>

<section id="likes">
  <h2>Likes</h2>
  <p>The like button on each article increments a simple, anonymous counter. We don't attach your identity to a like — we only use your browser's local storage to remember that you've already liked a specific article, so you can't click it repeatedly to inflate the count.</p>
</section>

<section id="cookies-storage">
  <h2>Cookies and local storage</h2>
  <p>We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. We use your browser's local storage (not a cookie) for one purpose only: remembering which articles you've already liked, so the button doesn't let you like the same article twice. This stays on your device and is never transmitted to us as personal data.</p>
</section>

<section id="third-party">
  <h2>Third-party services</h2>
  <p>This site is hosted on <b>Cloudflare</b>, and comments/likes are stored in Cloudflare's D1 database — both subject to Cloudflare's own privacy and data practices. We also load fonts from <b>Google Fonts</b>, which involves your browser making a request to Google's servers to display the site's typography.</p>
  <p>If we add analytics or advertising services in the future (such as Google Analytics or Google AdSense), we will update this policy to disclose them before they go live.</p>
</section>

<section id="your-rights">
  <h2>Your rights</h2>
  <p>You can request that we delete a comment you've posted at any time — contact us with a link to the comment and we'll remove it. Because comments aren't tied to an account, we rely on you identifying which comment is yours (e.g., by the article URL, your name as posted, and approximate date).</p>
</section>

<section id="children">
  <h2>Children's privacy</h2>
  <p>This site is intended for JAMB, WAEC, NECO, and Post-UTME candidates, many of whom are minors. We don't knowingly collect any information beyond what's described above, and we don't require any personal identifying information to read or interact with the site. Parents or guardians with concerns about a comment posted by a minor can contact us for removal.</p>
</section>
